Scope: the headers only (the shared Headers change). Bodies keep their own budgets.

- The task, the last check-in (time, hand), the unit, counts of pigs and pens, pen codes, the walk's recorder.

## Questions

1. Which unit am I walking? (the body's first row) 2. Can I trust the list (last check-in)? 3. What needs me? (the lens)

## Drafts (2026-10-14)

Back keeps a sheet's draft and Clear empties it. No fact is added, moved or cut: a reopened sheet shows the worker's own entry in the field it was typed in, and Clear is an action (the footer's quiet text, present only while something is entered), not a fact. Every first frame is unchanged.
