# Report missing 上报失踪

> Draft — compiled from existing docs, not yet confirmed.

## Problem

Production had two confirmation dialogs for reporting missing and captured no reason, date or photo. A missing report is a parking, not a deletion.

## Who

Anyone who cannot find a tagged pig; the manager who later resolves the list.

## Anchor

Anchor: The tagged pig, parked as missing.

## Rules

- Nothing is captured beyond the selection (no reason, date or photo); one load-bearing sentence says a report is a parking and the pig can be found in the missing list.
- A missing report parks an identity. An un-identified head has none, so a head that cannot be found is a wrong count and the fix is the pen's Count sheet. A group-only selection omits the verb; a mixed selection keeps it and scopes visibly.
- After commit the pig leaves her pen (booked as minus n), the pen count re-derives from placements and the feed sum follows. No discrepancy state.
- Her page outlives the report: the state line wears it, open cases stay open, history is whole, recovery is a one-line reversal.

## Scope

In: Report missing · drawer; Mixed selection · heads cannot go missing; After · rows leave, count re-derives; Her page · parked.

Out: see the neighbouring features of the same section for what the same sheet does elsewhere; nothing outside the screens above is designed here.

## Decisions

Open questions and items to confirm:

- Whether the pen count drops on report or only when the missing list resolves her (pen-count q3); production's formula never mentions reported-missing pigs.
- Where the missing list itself lives is unresolved (production named Toolbox > missing list and designed it nowhere; ambiguity 17).
