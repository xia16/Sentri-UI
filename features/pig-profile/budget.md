# pig-profile: headers, information budget

Scope: the headers only (the shared Headers change). Bodies keep their own budgets.

## Inventory

- The pig's tag, pen, unit, stage, days in stage, batch, counts of selected pigs, the finding's kind.

## Questions

1. What am I recording? (the title) 2. Is it the right pig? (the pig page, one Back away) 3. Can I save? (the primary)

## Levels

- No header line on any surface of this feature: every one is an action or a detail reached from the pig page or the walk, which show the pig. The pig page itself carries no line (its title is the tag; its pen is one Back away on the walk list).

## Headers (2026-10-13)

The header line of every surface of this feature, decided by the Headers law (`ux/design-system/README.md#headers-law-owner-2026-10-13`, owner 2026-10-13). The default is no line; each kept fact names its question and passes the Back test.
