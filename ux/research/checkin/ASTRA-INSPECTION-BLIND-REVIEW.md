# Inspection: independent visual reviews

Date: 13 September 2026

## Method

Two reviewers independently inspected the live prototype in separate browser tabs, without conversation history, implementation rationale or research notes. Scope: pig 000306 overview and complete profile, pig 000254, and Pen C2. Neither reviewer edited files or saved records. These reviews assess a prototype, not validated farm-floor usability.

## First review

Verdict: not yet acceptable for repeated farm-floor use.

- High: “Treat in place” was easy to overlook and duplicated across both condition notices.
- Medium: the feed qualification and actual equipment problem were visually subordinate to author/time metadata.
- Medium: the full profile was long, with repeated facts and large gaps.
- Low: “History” implied more than the current walk's records.

Changes made: common triage is displayed once as Care for N findings; differing triage remains with its case. Feed qualifications and fault descriptions receive dedicated prominent text. Profile facts are consolidated in a compact grid, with quiet View pen/View feed links. The history section is named Updates this walk.

## Fresh review of the revised prototype

Verdict: accept as a prototype. No high issues found.

The reviewer found clear separation between human-recorded findings, manual top-dressing and the automatic feed plan; the fault was specific and attributed; navigation was clear and density manageable. They noted some overview/detail repetition remains.

One medium issue remained: useful secondary text was too small and light. After this review, timing/attribution text was increased to 12 px, care instructions to 13 px, type tags to 11 px, and operational context was darkened. That final typography adjustment was checked locally rather than claimed as independently re-reviewed.

## Verification

The reading links retain 44 px tap height and return to the correct pig profile. Medium drawer heights are preserved. Layout fixtures cover 34 destinations plus feed/subject variants at 320 px and 390 px widths. Existing record-model and 400-pig selection/filter checks pass. The domain records and feed calculations were not changed by the visual redesign.
