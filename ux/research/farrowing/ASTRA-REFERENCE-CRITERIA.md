# Farrowing reference selection — owner constraint

11 September 2026. This selection rule supersedes the broad app sampling in the earlier Astra research rounds.

Use only established apps with substantial real adoption and strong design in the specific screen or flow being studied. A convenient search match, small specialist audience, polished promotional image or large parent company is insufficient. Popularity qualifies an app for inspection; it does not establish that every screen is well designed.

## Screen-level design test

Select a reference only when the actual screen has a clear primary task, legible hierarchy, coherent grouping, understandable state/action labels, and consistent controls. Explain exactly which visible quality is useful to farrowing. Inspect adjacent states for flow claims. Do not infer autosave, draft retention or usability performance from a screenshot.

Reject results from outside this shortlist unless the app first passes the same adoption and design checks. Search by app name to reduce irrelevant matches. A rejected query result is not a reference and should not drive the design.

### Initial screen checks under this rule

- [Airbnb guest selection](https://mobbin.com/screens/c2cfb78f-0842-4d97-930f-6e44b16765f3): accepted for the labeled-row grouping and stable minus/value/plus alignment. The screenshot shows several guest categories inside one active panel; it is not evidence of farrowing save semantics. Its small controls and decorative background treatment are not adopted.
- [Google Maps place detail](https://mobbin.com/screens/5b38f4ff-989f-4634-a8e6-1072bbd2d9ab): accepted for visible parent context, compact place identity and one emphasized action in a bottom sheet. The horizontal action carousel and photo content are not relevant to farrowing.
- [Uber returned result](https://mobbin.com/screens/9228af44-f568-4f96-a70c-23023f075e7a): rejected for the requested completed-trip comparison. It actually shows hourly-ride terms with Confirm, not a completed trip or receipt. Uber's scale does not make this a relevant result.
- Previously inspected AllTrails edit-sheet and Strava summary examples remain eligible for their specific visible hierarchy. Revolut remains eligible for focused record/status comparisons, not for importing financial transaction logic.

## Effect on previous rounds

The prior reports remain a research history, not an approved reference library. Bevel, GO Club, MacroFactor, Crouton, Alta, Slopes and other specialist or unqualified apps are excluded from the design benchmark. ODK/SafetyCulture operational notes remain historical background and will not set the visual direction or expand the product flow. Other previously returned apps are also outside the current shortlist unless explicitly qualified later; absence from this shortlist is not a claim about their actual popularity.

Recommendations supported by local owner decisions or accessibility standards remain valid on those grounds. Recommendations relying on an excluded app need support from the qualified set before being adopted. The existing farrowing logic and sheet workflow remain the constraints.
