Farrowing · 分娩

Thesis: farrowing is one live, multi-session record — five counters, an interval clock, and a “continue vs final” decision. Production wraps that record in three tabs, a grid, a legend, an end-of-task ceremony and a read-only archive module; our list + live-record sheet already carry everything except the interval engine’s exact rules, the birth-time field, and the sow-card side actions (foster · death · abortion · mark).

Deltas — the only farrowing-specific design

DeltaSpec
Δ1 Interval engineBands: ≥45 min amber, ≥60 min red (config). Banner on record page; same chip on the list row. Dismiss kills the current band only; engine keeps counting and re-raises at the next band. No last-birth time on the latest record → engine silent. Never modal.
Δ2 Last-birth timeOne optional field: “last birth ≈ N min ago”. The only manual time in the task; everything else derives from record timestamps.
Δ3 Session trail in-pageThe record page lists prior sessions (live/dead · who · when) under the steppers — replaces the separate 分娩详情 page and the archive sheet; read-only after final.
Δ4 Record-page overflow[···] with state gates: pre-farrow Mark · Abortion; from first record + Foster · Report death · Count check. Foster-out requires receiver sow; foster-in source sow or “unknown”. These write the piglet ledger that explains “(N fostered)”.
Δ5 Self-close semanticsEvent task: no End button anywhere on the phone. Sow leaves the list by final or abortion; window-end disposal of never-farrowed sows (batch removal + typed-count confirm) moves to console.

6 · Open questions

QuestionWhy it matters
1 · Who closes the window on never-farrowed sows?Production makes the phone user remove them from the batch with a typed count. If that moves to console (Δ5), define who and when — and whether abortion is the only pen-side exit.
2 · Farrowed sow still listed in pregnancy-check (annotation 910:12121)Bug or intent? If intent, the preg-check task carries dead weight; our derivation rules would auto-remove her.
3 · Interval thresholds — 30 or 45/60?Production shows 30 (grid, filter) and 45/60 (banner). One config, but the default values need an owner.
4 · Capture assistance / oxytocin?Production captures neither. If the farm wants intervention data (manual extraction, oxytocin dose — standard in farrowing management), it is a new field on the record page, not a redesign.
5 · 未定位 sows — record in place or locate first?Already open in the unified spec (§4.5); farrowing makes it acute: a sow farrowing “nowhere” still needs her litter recorded.
6 · Weak/deformed piglets — do they need follow-up flags?Production counts them then forgets them. If weak counts should seed piglet-treatment attention, that is a spawn rule to confirm.
