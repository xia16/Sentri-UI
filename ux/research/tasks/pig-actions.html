Pig actions — entry points, bulk, scanning

Everything a farmer does to a pig that is not the task in hand. This doc settles three things at the system level: where these actions are launched from, which of them work on more than one pig, and what scanning does on each surface. The inside of each form is deliberately out of scope — those pages come later; what has to be right now is that a farmer always knows where to reach for them.

Two findings that shape the model.
1 · There is no 日常巡检 flow in the file — the string appears zero times. What exists is 猪只列表 (2512:9216): a house-scoped, health-oriented pig list with a batch bar and its own ⋯, filed as a context screen under a deferred module. That list is the ad-hoc surface; it was never named one.
2 · 更多操作 is not one menu. 7 rows in 分娩/仔猪处理, 4 rows in 查情 (1876:15130), gated by task stage (rule at 1649:20977). That is precisely why 母猪流产 appears in piglet processing, where it cannot apply.

The sprawl being replaced

Six surfaces, two menu vocabularies, three entry points for the (now cut) Mark action, three names for Transfer, and two destinations referenced but designed nowhere (健康管理, 批量编辑病例). Nothing in production tells a farmer which surface holds which action.

2 · Decisions taken

DecisionConsequence
Mark is cut entirely CUTThe action, its sheet, its three entry points, the red dot on rows and pen cells, the 已被标记 row line, and the "flagged" filter chip all go. Its only schema was one optional free-text note whose real uses — "she looks unwell", "her tag is damaged" — are a health record and a data-quality issue respectively. Accepted loss: production's free-note capability disappears; if a note with no clinical meaning proves necessary, it returns as a field on the health record, not as its own action.
Abortion stays gatedDisabled when the sow is not bred, with the reason shown. No "record anyway" override — an abortion with no mating on file is a data problem to fix upstream, and an active-looking row that then argues with you is worse than a quiet one.
Count check keeps its toolbox rowBut it lands in the litter record's count-verify step rather than being a second standalone form. Rationale below.
Per-task menu variants CUTOne set, gated on the pig (§5), so nothing can leak into a task where it cannot apply.
Two Treatment designs MERGEOne form; "batch" is the same form over a selection made beforehand, so production's step-1 picker disappears.
Four fostering step-1 screens MERGEOne counterpart-picker parameterised by direction and scope.
猪只列表's private batch bar MERGEIts three actions are the same actions in bulk; batch belongs to a list with a selection (§4).
Why Count check lands in the litter record. Death, abortion, transfer and fostering are events in an animal's life. A count mismatch is not an event — it is a correction to a record that already exists (the litter's head count). If the toolbox owned its own count form, two different screens could change the same number and they would eventually disagree. So the toolbox row stays — noticing a mismatch while walking is exactly an ad-hoc moment — but it opens the litter record's count-verify step. One implementation, two doors.

4 · Individual vs bulk

Three patterns, decided by what the payload is — not by which screen you happen to be on.

ActionBulk?PatternWhy
TreatmentYesShared payloadOne drug, dose and method applied to N pigs — the form is filled once. A whole pen treated for the same thing is the normal case, and production already built 批量治疗.
TransferYesShared payloadOne destination for N pigs. Moving a group is the normal case (production: 批量转舍).
Health recordYesHand-offProduction's 批量编辑病例. The health module owns what happens next.
Report deathYesPer-subject payloadN pigs selected together, but each carries its own cause and photos — the flow ends in a per-pig list. Bulk selection, individual detail.
Report abortionNo—Each abortion has its own cause, and each removes that sow from her batch. A bulk version would apply one reason to several animals and quietly restructure a batch.
Foster pigletsNo—The subject is a pair of sows; the multiplicity lives in the piglets moved, not in the pigs acted on. Selecting several sows has no meaning.
Count checkNo—Corrects one litter's record.
Bulk is offered by absence, not by disabling. On the selection bar the individual-only actions simply do not appear. Disabled rows are for things that could apply to this pig but do not yet (§5); an action that is never a group action is not a gap in the menu, it is not part of that menu at all.
Bulk never crosses the batch. A selection may span pens and rooms — a farmer treating a row of sick pigs does not care about pen walls. But Report death and Transfer both change where an animal belongs, so the confirmation step always restates the selection grouped by pen, and any pig whose state would block the action is listed separately rather than silently skipped.

5 · Scanning

Scan is the identity resolver, not an action. It answers "which pig", and what happens next belongs to the surface you are on.

SurfaceScan doesNotes
Task list (dock)Jumps to her row, pen header pinned, and ticks her — or opens her sheet in tasks that have no checkboxAlready designed (task-screens 04b). Unchanged here.
Check-in listAdds her to the selectionScope is not changed — production states this for death: scanning does not switch the selected house.
Picker inside a flow
death · foster		Adds her to the selectionSame rule; the flow's own scope stands.
Identity field in a formFills the tagFoster's identity supplement, piglet tag/weigh.
Fallback: ear notchManual picker — two tappable ear diagrams (2952:34476)For pigs with no electronic tag; error state when the notch is unknown.
Three scanning rules. 1 · Scan never changes scope. Not the house, not the lens, not the filter. It resolves a pig inside what you are already looking at, and says so if she is outside it. 2 · Scan is additive. It adds to a selection; it never replaces one. Scanning ten pigs in a row is the fast path for bulk treatment. 3 · Every scan reports what it resolved — a toast naming the tag (production: 000001 猪只已添加). A scan that changes nothing visible is a scan the farmer cannot trust.

Because scanning is additive and scope-preserving, it is also the answer to bulk selection with gloves: rather than hunting checkboxes, a farmer scans each pig he treats and then opens the action once. Scan and the selection bar are the same mechanism seen from two ends.

7 · Check-in — the ad-hoc list

Production's 猪只列表 is already the right shape: house-scoped, searchable by tag or notch, pen groups with group checkboxes, health line per row, scan. Three changes make it part of the system:

ChangeWhy
It is the task list component, minus the taskNo lens (no task state to partition), no task overview cell. Same pen grouping, dock, pen picker, peek.
Health is the row's subject, not a decorationTask rows state the task's answer and nothing else — so health leaves them. Here health is the reason the screen exists, so it is line 1.
Selection bar = the bulk-capable actionsTreatment · Transfer · Report death, plus the health hand-off. Same forms as the sheet, N subjects.

This is the surface for the case that started this: a worker walking with no task in hand, who sees a symptom or an abortion and records it. Tapping a row gives him the same sheet and the same ⋯ he would get inside any production task — which is the entire point.

9 · Open questions

1 · Treatment vs Health record. Production has both, with no stated boundary. Proposal: Treatment records an intervention; Health record opens the case and the health module owns diagnosis. Confirm.

2 · Death cause optional. Production allows submitting with no cause behind a warning dialog. Mortality is a KPI on the task detail sheet — confirm whether an unknown cause is acceptable indefinitely, or must be resolved later from the console.

3 · Transfer's missing design. The action exists in the 查情 menu and the pig-list batch bar but has no screens in the 更多操作 section; the 3-screen sub-flow in 查情 is the only reference. Confirm it is the intended design before we build on it.

4 · Toolbox contents beyond pigs. Production's 工具箱 carries a red to-do for a death-record event list. If the toolbox is also where records are browsed (not just started), that is a second job for the same surface and worth naming now.
