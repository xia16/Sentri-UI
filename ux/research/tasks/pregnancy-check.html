Pregnancy check · 孕检

A three-outcome sweep — pregnant / not pregnant / unclear — where every outcome is a routing decision the system already knows how to make: pregnant follows the batch to farrowing, unclear schedules its own recheck, not-pregnant leaves the batch for rebreeding. Production spends five screens and two confirmations narrating those consequences; the optimized task states each consequence once, at the moment of the mark, and everything else is derived.

Deltas (all that actually needed design)

D1Recheck as a default, not a question. Unclear radio carries → recheck in 3 d from config; tapping the hint opens a small stepper for the exception. Kills the mandatory dialog while keeping the per-sow override production wanted.
D2Consequence hints on the radios. → follows batch · farrowing / → recheck in 3 d / → leaves batch · rebreed. This is where the submit page, the legend, and the end-task detail all collapse to — said once, at the decision.
D3Sheet ⋯ drawer: Add marker · Record death. New system-wide affordance (every task except farrowing and piglet processing, per the designer note).
D4End-task summary rows. The detail sheet's End task (03) gains the three consequence tallies — production's 执行详情, minus the typed-count ritual.
D5Ear notch (耳缺号) as an alternate search key in dock search and history — production's ended browser already treats it as an identity.

6 · Open questions

Q1When does batch removal commit? Production defers it to submit/end-task; our marks commit themselves. Does "not pregnant" move the sow out of the batch (and into rebreed) at mark time, or does the herd move wait for End task while the record commits immediately? Mark-time is our default — confirm the herd-management side tolerates immediate moves with ✎-corrections.
Q2Who owns a recheck that falls outside the 3-day window? Unclear +3 d can land after the sweep ends. Does she surface in the next scheduled check task, or keep a standing recheck row that survives End task? (End task currently reports her as "follows batch, pending".)
Q3Recheck default value and override. Is 3 d the farm standard (production's own row copy suggests yes)? And is a per-sow override needed at all, or is D1's stepper dead weight we can drop later?
Q4Number of check rounds. Config shows 1st (d10) and 2nd (d20). Fixed at two, or N configurable rounds — and does a sow confirmed pregnant in the 1st round still appear in the 2nd?
Q5Ear notch as first-class ID. Production searches by it only in the ended browser. Do farms actually key on notch in daily work (gilts before tagging?) — if yes it belongs in dock search everywhere, not just here.
