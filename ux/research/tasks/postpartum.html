Postpartum check · 母猪产后检查

6 · Open questions

1Recheck trigger + interval. Production shows the state (需复查：x 天后) but never says which results schedule it — any abnormal item? only some (e.g. mastitis ≥ moderate)? — and whether x is per-farm config.
2Pass definition for 产后合格率. Zero abnormal items? BCS within 2–4? Excludes cull-recommended? The KPI is on every screen but the formula is nowhere.
3Mastitis taxonomy. Blank form has 无/轻度/中度/重度; filled and read-only states render only three (无 missing). Confirm 4 values, with 无 as an explicit recorded answer (it should be — “not asked” ≠ “none”).
4Required fields. 编辑态 shows submit enabled with BCS unselected. Are all nine answers mandatory per check, or is partial save legal? Is cull reason mandatory when recommending?
5Early-end carry-over. 继续跟随批次: do unchecked sows re-enter the next round/batch task, or is the miss permanent in compliance? Numbers on 4347:4469 don’t add up (100+100→100), so the intended assertion needs stating.
6Ended-task audit filters. Abnormal-item / cull-rec / last-handled-date slicing — keep on the phone’s ended view, or is that a reporting-surface job?
