# Health management

Figma references: More actions `2501:6101`, disease picker `2632:16389`, symptom picker `2803:16252`, and treatment `2632:16590`.

## Intent

Keep findings, care instructions, treatment, and resolution connected without forcing the farmer to understand separate disease and symptom storage models.

## Current coverage

The shared sow Actions surface already includes:

- one searchable disease/symptom catalogue;
- health findings for one or many pigs;
- care instructions such as monitor, treat in place, and hospital pen;
- treatment linked to an optional active condition;
- recovery and correction paths;
- unavailable actions with visible reasons.

Farrowing should continue to open these shared flows rather than duplicate them inside the litter task.
