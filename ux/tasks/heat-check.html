Our law: actions commit themselves, rounds are ambient, nothing is submitted on a room screen. The counts are the lens counts; the rosters are the "In heat" lens and the task overview (03). The one real assertion it carried — "unchecked defaults to not-in-heat" — is simply dropped: unmarked sows keep their state, and no negative is ever asserted, silently or per pen (pen headers aggregate, they do not attest). 6 · Open questions

1 · Unchecked semantics. Production bulk-defaults every unchecked sow to "not in heat" at submit. Our model asserts nothing: unmarked sows keep their state, and recorded check activity is the compliance evidence. Confirm that this evidence model satisfies the compliance requirement — no per-pen or per-sow negative confirmations exist by design.
2 · End-task guard. Removing 200 sows from a batch is destructive; production arms the button by making the worker retype the count. Keep a typed guard, or is one explicit confirm naming the consequence enough with gloves on?
3 · Breeding handoff timing. Production moves in-heat sows to Breeding "after submit". With ambient rounds there is no submit — does the sow join Breeding the moment she is marked (and leave this cohort immediately), or at round close?
4 · Archive surface. Ended-task module: fold into farm reports, or does the barn phone need per-task history at all?
5 · Transfer scope. The 2026.1.23 flow is single-pig only. Is bulk transfer (whole pen after weaning) a real need, or does single-pig cover the heat-check context?
