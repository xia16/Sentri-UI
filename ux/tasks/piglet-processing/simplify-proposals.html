Piglet processing · design review

Seven ways to make it simpler

Three rounds of walk-throughs kept finding edge cases, and each fix added another screen, question or number. This page shows, for each source of that complexity, what a worker does today and what they would do under a simpler rule. Each rule is stricter, and each one removes whole screens.

The rules behind every proposal

  • One way to do each thing. Record death, Move and Set count are the only ways the number of piglets changes.
  • The app settles conflicts itself. No worker is ever asked to resolve another phone's record.
  • One schedule per litter. A piglet that moves follows its new litter's day.
  • Ask only what the worker knows at that moment, with Yes / No answers.
  • After End, nothing records.

1 · Moving piglets

Recommend: simplify

By “cohorts” I didn't mean the farm's batch. I meant piglets of different ages living in one crate after a foster, which today each keep their own schedule. If fostering stays inside the batch (sows that farrowed within the same few days), every piglet in a crate can follow that crate's day.

Today

  1. B09 → Move → pick B02 (any litter, any age) → pick 3 piglets.
  2. “Iron: did all 3 have it?” Yes / No / Don't know.
  3. With Don't know, B09 loses its one-tap and shows a range.
  4. B02 shows the arrivals as their own group with their own day.
  5. Someone later opens B02, checks spray marks, answers “already had 1 · record 2”, which narrows B09.
B09 Iron · 0–2 of 7 owed · check B02 Iron · 11 of its own · day 3 Iron · 3 from B09 · day 4 · unknown

Proposed

  1. B09 → Move → pick B02 (only litters in the same batch are listed) → pick 3 piglets.
  2. “Iron: had it?” Yes / No — the spray mark from treatment tells the worker.
  3. Save. The 3 piglets now follow B02's day; any “No” is owed in B02's normal Iron row.
B09 Iron · 2 owed · Record 2 B02 Iron · 14 owed · Record 14
What you give upFostering across batches, and moving piglets without knowing what they had. Workers must spray-mark at treatment so the move question is always answerable.

2 · When the count doesn't match

Recommend: your idea, below

Your suggestion: Move and Record death are explicit buttons, and a count difference is a quick report that doesn't interrupt the work.

Today

  1. Set count 11 (record says 12) → Save writes an “unexplained loss” line.
  2. The room shows “Not yet explained”; the litter gets a Check chip.
  3. An Explain page lists suggestions (“A04 gained 1 · both lines close”), a Review move, a Move sheet, and “in another crate”.
  4. Losses and gains stay open until paired, recounted through Edit, or explained by a death.

Proposed

  1. Set count 11 → one quiet choice under the number: Not sure why (default) · Count was wrong.
  2. Count was wrong just corrects the number. Not sure why saves and the litter row says “1 missing”.
  3. Later, Record death on that litter asks one thing: “One of the missing?” Move from it asks the same, already ticked.
  4. A high count raises Alive and says “check on the pig”. No pairing, no explain page.
A02 Iron · Tail · 11 owed · 1 missing
What you give upThe app no longer suggests that one litter's loss is another's gain. If a piglet jumped next door, the worker records it as a Move.

3 · Two phones, same thing (offline)

Recommend: first in wins

Your idea fits. Here is how git does it, and why our case is easier.

How git worksEach person works on a branch. Merging combines both. Changes to different lines merge automatically; only the same line changed two ways is a conflict, and git stops and asks a person, because it can't guess code.
Why ours is easierEach record is a fact added to a log, like a commit. Almost nothing conflicts: two deaths, a move and a treatment simply all apply. Real conflicts are rare and the app can settle them by rule.
The ruleFor the same thing recorded twice, the first record to reach the farm counts. The second is kept as a note, never counted, and its author's phone says so. Nobody is asked to answer anything.

4 · A nurse sow outside the task

Recommend: cut

Today

  1. A sow dies; Move her piglets to E01, a sow that isn't in the processing task.
  2. The preview warns “E01's own 7 piglets join too · owe Cut cord, Nasal drops (2 days late)”.
  3. E01 joins the task; her own piglets now owe everything on their own day; her earlier litter's facts sit on a separate line.

Proposed

  1. Move lists only litters already in this task.
  2. To use E01, the supervisor adds her litter to the task first (one step in the task overview). Then she is an ordinary litter.
What you give upA worker can't foster onto a sow outside the task on the spot; it takes the supervisor's one step.

5 · Records that arrive after End

Recommend: cut

Today

  1. The supervisor ends the task at 16:20.
  2. An offline phone syncs a treatment done at 17:05.
  3. It is kept, flagged “after end · not counted”, and listed in a “Since end” section on the receipt and the litter.
  4. A mark stamped before End but synced after is “accepted”, and the receipt explains it.

Proposed

  1. After End, nothing records.
  2. A phone that syncs late shows its author: “Not saved · the task ended at 16:20.”
  3. Receipt and handoff stay frozen; no Since-end section.
What you give upThe record of work done after End. A worker whose late record was refused tells the supervisor.

6 · Fine-grained corrections

Recommend: cut

Today

  1. A death of 2 bodies → Edit → tick which body was a mistake.
  2. A short treatment → “Some weak, some sick” → split the count between reasons.
  3. A castration → hernia / cryptorchid / deferred / kept boar, each with its own count.

Proposed

  1. Edit withdraws or moves a whole record, then the worker records the right one again.
  2. A short treatment takes one reason: Weak or Sick.
  3. Castration keeps its reasons (your ruling), one reason per record.
What you give upFixing half a record in one step; it becomes withdraw and re-record (two steps, rarely needed).

7 · Recording several litters at once

Recommend: keep

Today

  2. Tick litters (litters already done, deferred or not due are left out).
  3. Review → hold to record 46 piglets.

If cut

Why keep itIt is one screen, it saves the most time on a day-3 round, and walk-throughs found it safe. The only change: add Undo after the hold, like the one-tap Record.
