# Piglet processing: skipped treatments, castration, identity schemes. Real-world practice

This extends `domain.md` (A1–A6) and does not repeat it. Every claim is tagged **sourced** (with a
URL) or **inferred** (with the reasoning). "Search digest" means the claim came from a search-engine
summary of a page that could not be fetched directly. Treat those claims as weaker. `eval-set.md` was
not opened.

**The short answer to the owner's question.** A piglet whose iron was skipped *does* get iron
later. Weak piglets are the textbook case: Chinese guidance delays their iron to day 3–5 on purpose.
Most farms also have a second iron round at about day 14–17 that catches any piglet missed in the
first round. Among the routine items, only the *timing-bound* ones stop being worth catching up once
their window closes: teeth, tail, the nasal vaccine and toltrazuril. Castration of a hernia or
cryptorchid male is *deferred* to a different person, a different method and a later age (15–60 d),
so it leaves the suckling-processing task. It is not caught up inside the task.

## 1 · Skipped treatments: what happens to the piglet that was not done

### 1.2 Skip reasons by treatment

| Reason | Teeth | Tail | Iron | Toltraz. | Castrate | Nasal | ID |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weak / small | defer or skip **(s)** | defer (i) | **defer to d3–5 (s)** | give (i) | **defer (s)** | give if able (i) | skip if death likely (i) |
| Sick | defer (i) | defer (i) | defer (i) | give; therapy (i) | **defer (s)** | **skip, catch up later (s)** | — |
| Hernia (scrotal/inguinal) | — | — | — | — | **exclude → surgery at 15–60 d (s)** | — | breeding candidate lost (s: heritable) |
| Cryptorchid | — | — | — | — | **exclude → re-check or surgery at 35–60 d (s)** | — | candidate lost (s) |
| Kept for breeding | — | — | — | — | **never (s)** | — | **identified (s)** |
| Fostered in, already processed | teeth/tail visible → skip (i) | same | **invisible → risk of double dose (i)** | invisible | visible | invisible | notch/tag visible |
| Died | ends every due item; counts as a death, not a skip (i) | | | | | | |

(s) = sourced in §1.1 · (i) = inferred.

**The design point about visibility (inferred, from the table).** Teeth, tail, castration and
identity show on the piglet. Iron, toltrazuril, the nasal vaccine and 保健 do not. For the invisible
items, the only thing that tells a hand whether a given piglet was done is a **spray mark** or a
record. That is exactly why the risk of double iron and the risk of missing a fostered piglet both
sit on the invisible items. The Teagasc foster kit carries a spray marker (domain.md A4).

treatment as a **count per litter** with product and dose, never as a bool. The exception (hernia)
is a **separate defect/condition fact**, not a reason attached to the treatment. That is richer than
production's bool and simpler than per-piglet rows.

### 2.2 Exceptions that matter, with their fates

| Exception | How found | Fate | Leaves the task? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scrotal / inguinal hernia | Palpation at the catch; that is why castration waits until ≥day 3–4 | Special surgery later (15–60 d), or left entire and sold as a hernia pig | Yes: different method, person and age |
| Cryptorchid (one or both) | Only one testis felt | Mark; re-check for descent; else cryptorchidectomy (35–60 d) or sold entire | Yes |
| Weak / small / sick | Condition at the catch | **Deferred**: castrated at a later pass in the same suckling period | **No**: stays due |
| Kept as a boar candidate | Farm selection (breeding farms only) | Never castrated | Yes: permanently not applicable |
| Died | — | Removed from due | Yes: via a death record |
| Farm does not castrate (EU entire males, immunocastration) | Policy | Castration not in the schedule at all | Config |

Sources as in §1.1 (castration row); entire-male policies: VT *Castration of Pigs*
https://www.sites.ext.vt.edu/newsletter-archive/livestock/aps-08_01/aps-0111.html (Norway and
Switzerland bans, Dutch retail). The split into "leaves the task" and "stays due" is **inferred**
from the fates.

### 2.3 What a record better than a "done" button holds (inferred, built from the sources above)

`castrate · litter L · day D · by W`
- **castrated: n** (a count, like PigCHAMP's "Number of Piglets")
- **not castrated, with fate**: `hernia k` · `cryptorchid k` · `deferred (weak/sick) k` · `kept boar k`
- *derived*: **males = castrated + Σ not castrated**. This becomes the litter's first reliable sex
  count on farms that do not sex at birth, and it cross-checks the birth ♂ count where one exists.
- *residual due* = `deferred` only. Hernia and cryptorchid become a **defect/condition** on the
  litter (PigCHAMP's Piglet Defects shape), which a vet or surgery follow-up can pick up. Kept boars
  are permanently not applicable.
- Product and dose do not apply. The **method** (knife/scalpel; anaesthesia yes/no) is what GAP
  and EU rules ask for, and it can come from farm config.

The same pattern generalises to every treatment: **count done + count excepted by reason**. The
reason decides whether the residual stays due (deferred) or closes (not applicable / died /
referred). This is ladder.md's `7 of 12` with the residual split by fate, instead of every
residual staying due.

### 3.2 How a farm moves from notch (or nothing) to tag

- **In software, it is an explicit selection event**: 猪场管家 *猪只选种* turns a meat pig into a
  breeding pig. It registers the ear number, **dam's ear number (optional)**, date, pen, sex and
  average weight, and moves the animal from meat stock to breeding stock. It is used "for
  replacement gilts without ear numbers, tagged at mating" and "when selected from nursery or finisher
  pens". PigCHAMP's equivalent is *Gilt Retained* (the farm's unique ID ≤15 chars + genetics).
  — **sourced**: http://help.gxswine.com/doku.php?id=pigfarm7.0:scgl_zzgl_zzyz ; PigCHAMP guide p. 14.
- 猪场管家 *种猪进场* says outright that an immature replacement without an ear number is managed as
  **meat-pig stock until selected**. — **sourced**: …:scgl_zzgl_zzjc.
- On the floor, the notch is read and written onto the tag ("耳牌上使用油性记号笔写耳号"). If a
  tag is lost, the number is recovered "通过种猪卡或耳缺". One tag per pig, not two. — **sourced**:
  Sohu GP 701348904.
- **Consequence (inferred).** The link from a suckling piglet to its later breeding ID is *optional*
  in both products (dam ID is optional in 猪只选种). Farms only get per-piglet lineage if the piglet
  was identified in the farrowing house (schemes B, C, E, F).

### 3.3 What a farm must configure so the app knows what "identity done" means (inferred)

| Setting | Values | What "identity work complete for a litter" means |
|---|---|---|
| Scheme | none · notch-all · tag-all · notch-then-tag · candidates-only · parentage-notch | none: nothing is ever due. All: identified = alive. Candidates-only: identified = candidates chosen |
| Mark type(s) at processing | notch · visual tag · RFID · notch+tag | Which fields a piglet row needs (SYNTHESIS: notch-only is legal) |
| Who is eligible | all · gilts only · all from flagged litters (GP/dam-line sows) · hand's choice | Eligibility may come from the **sow** (a GP-line flag), not the piglet |
| Numbering | litter + pig (notch, both conventions) · farm-sequential · odd/even by sex · free text | Drives the +1 hint and sex inference from odd/even |
| Day | processing day · weaning · selection (outside task) | Whether identity is a processing item at all |
| Completion rule | **all alive** · **the hand declares the candidate set closed** ("9 of 20, done") | On keepers-only farms 9/20 is complete once the hand closes the set. The residual 11 are *not applicable*, not *due* |

The keepers-only case is therefore a *declared-set* completion, the same shape as castration's
"not castrated · kept boar". Identity and castration are complements on breeding farms: the males
kept as boars are the ones identified.

**Takeaway (inferred).** Every piglet-level item is *given* per piglet but *recorded* per litter as a
count. Only whole-pen items (iron soil, creep) are truly litter-level. So a "whole litter" tick is a
shorthand for "count = alive", and an exception is a smaller count.

## 5 · What changes versus domain.md and the repo

1. **The partial treatment is a count with fates, not a bool** (settles domain.md C2 in the
   direction of ladder.md, but refines it). Real software stores `n treated`. A residual needs a
   **reason that decides whether it stays due**: *deferred (weak/sick)* → due; *not applicable (kept
   boar / keepers-only ID)* → closed; *referred (hernia/cryptorchid)* → closed here, open as a
   condition; *died* → a death record.
2. **Iron is the one treatment with a sanctioned deferral inside the task (weak → d3–5)** and a
   farm-level safety net (second iron at d14–17). "Overdue stays overdue" (SYNTHESIS §7.8) fits iron
   well, but fits teeth, tail, toltrazuril and nasal badly: they should *lapse* at a configurable age.
3. **Castration exceptions leave the task**, so "castrate" can never reach "all males" on a litter
   with a hernia unless the exception is recorded. A plain bool either lies or blocks.
4. **Visible vs invisible items** is the real axis for fostered-in piglets and double-dosing:
   iron, toltrazuril and nasal need a mark (spray or record); tail, teeth, castration and ID
   self-evidence.
5. **Product and dose per head** belong in the farm config and are **snapshotted into the count
   record** (猪场管家 books vaccine consumption as count × dose), which answers domain.md B10#9 /
   C3.
6. **The sex count has three sources**: birth ♂/♀ (breeding farms, paper card), identity rows
   (odd/even notch, or a sex field), and **castration's own total** (castrated + excepted). Commercial
   farms often have only the third.
