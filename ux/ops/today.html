Today — the front page

The only scheduling surface, scoped to a section, with one scope rail: Overview — the day's tasks — or any single unit, the same surface narrowed to that unit's own overview. A task is one question over one batch; two pregnancy batches are two tasks, and a room they share costs one walk because tasks transition inside the unit. And one law is new here, learned the hard way: planning surfaces are cards; execution surfaces are rows. The pig list compresses two hundred animals into row grammar because density is its job. Today holds four decisions — each gets a card with room to breathe, one primary fact, and nothing borrowed from the list.

The card, and why it is not a row

Planning surfaces are cards; execution surfaces are rows. Row grammar (01b) exists so two hundred animals survive one screen — 13px type, two lines, everything abbreviated. Today is the opposite problem: four decisions that deserve deliberation. So the task gets a card: a 17px name, its batch spelled out in words, a full-width progress mark, and one fact pair. The pig list's density tricks — tag columns, token lines, packed rails — are banned here. The two surfaces should not look like siblings, because they are not: one is read standing still, the other mid-walk.
One instrument per card. The focal point is a single 27px numeral read as a phrase — 100 of 200 checked · 180 to check · day 3 of 7 — with one quiet secondary fact opposite it (window ends sat · 2 returned · attribution). Mono is spent only on the numeral; every word around it is sans. Anything that doesn't change what the worker does next was cut: no work-group breakdowns, no pen counts, no percentages, no batch chips-in-boxes — the batch is just words.
Status is a pill, and it is the card's only colored element. Soft wash, colored text, glyph inside — ● Due now — so color is contained instead of shouting from naked letterspaced caps. The bar repeats the same hue once. The overdue count leaves the text entirely: it rides the bar as a thin red segment, and the strip above says it in words. One card, one hue, said twice quietly — the strip is the only place on the screen allowed to shout, which is the exception-first law drawn as typography.
Cards shrink as they cool. A done task drops its bar and compresses; a watch task dims its ground and shows day segments instead of a bar. The eye lands on the biggest, brightest card — which is always the one that needs a decision.

What unit scope is

RegisterCarries
The unit's overviewThe census large, its batch composition in words, last-checked with a name. Two doors: Pigs › — the unit list, every animal, verb sheet behind any selection (06) — and Check-in, the stamp (entrance, not a task, never gated). The attention chips are the pooled care work
Its task cardsThe card gains its breakdown in unit scope: the imperative with the count as the loud row (Check · 40), then the reasons-to-act beneath it — first check due · 8, re-check · 4. Every row is a door: the card body opens the task's list here; a reason row opens it with that lens already set. Two pregnancy batches are two cards here too
StripUnit-scoped: only this unit's escalations; G4 has none, so none renders
DockUnchanged — pen · scan · search. Scope never moves the doors
The transition is the point. Finish a task's work here and its list's end state offers the next open task in this unit — Next · Pregnancy check B22 · 12 here. The phone swaps the question; the worker never leaves the room. Two batch tasks sharing a room cost one walk because the merge happens by being in the unit, never by blending lists. Order: due → open, worst first; a per-farm sequence is an open question.
The breakdown is the lens, surfaced — and it is the fix for the metadata problem. An earlier draft wrote 40 here to check as a small fact line — a statement about the world. The live UI's unit cards (08i) state the same number as an execution overview: the action, its count, and why now, bucketed — reached the window, showing signs, re-check due. Those buckets are exactly the list's lens partition (01b), so the card invents no taxonomy: it surfaces the lens counts as tappable reasons. Action first, evidence under it, metadata gone.
Round tasks rest between rounds instead of playing done. From the live UI's finest detail: after the AM heat round, the card neither disappears nor shouts ✓ — it rests, holding the two facts a worker plans by: Heat check✓ AM round 46 of 46 · 09:12 · G.H Next round · 14:30in 4 h 48 m
Care work becomes cards only here. Treatments and measures never reach Overview — they pool where the animals are and surface as this unit's cards and chips, executed task-at-a-time like everything else.
Never a merged multi-task pig list. The pig list behind Pigs › is the ad-hoc surface — selection → verb sheet, any event recordable, watching tasks advance by construction. A lens partitions by one question; bulk marks are only safe when the whole selection answers it.

Laws of the route

One decision per row: which room next. The route strips itself to the walk — room, its pens, its count. The count is the right edge's big numeral (a route ranks by how much, so the number outranks a status word here); the one annotation allowed is co-located work, stated as a fact: B22 also due here. Everything else — group panels, per-room progress bars, status previews — was cut.
The rhythm: route → room → next. Enter a room; its list is the unit-scoped task list (01). When it runs dry the end state offers Next unit · G4 — or Next task here first if this room holds more work (08c). Sweep or stay: both offers, one tap, at every room's end.
No "Close unit round", no "Review & complete". Rooms close themselves when their work set is empty; the task is ✓ when its window closes. Wireframe 02's three-screen ceremony certified what the event model derives — consolidation dissolved it.
Single-unit tasks skip this screen — the card opens its list directly. The route exists only where there is a route.

What qualifies

Line 1 is the fact; line 2 is the address; the rail is always ▲ OVERDUE. A derived view, not an inbox: rows leave when the fact resolves, never by dismissal. When empty, the register does not render. The strip alone keeps row grammar — an exception is triage, and triage reads like a list.

The philosophy, stated once

PrincipleWhere it lands
The overview is the task viewOne card per task × batch — the day's plan, one primary fact each, readable without a tap
One surface, two scopesThe scope rail toggles Overview ⇄ unit; scope changes the question, never the grammar. Filtering changes the scope, not ownership
Cards plan; rows executeToday deliberates in cards; the lists behind it compress in rows. The two densities never mix on one surface — the strip's triage rows are the single exception
Sweep or enter — the worker choosesEquipment in hand → task card → its route. Standing in a room → its chip → the unit's overview, tasks taken one at a time
Ad-hoc has an entrance, not a menuUnit scope's Pigs › and Check-in, and the dock's scan. Today itself never records anything
What Today is not. Not a dashboard (herd stats belong to a farm surface with the toolbox). Not an inbox. Not a report. A work order that happens to be calm.
