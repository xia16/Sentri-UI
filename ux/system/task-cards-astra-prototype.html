FIXED COMPONENT FIELDS

  1. 01

    Identity

    Task name and batch or plan. A compact clock + Day N of N badge replaces “In progress”.

  2. 02

    What needs attention

    Show the highest-priority actionable fact. With no work due, show the next scheduled event quietly. Finished work invites review and completion. Ending early remains a separate outcome.

  3. 03

    Progress, with its meaning

    Whole-task progress stays visible while waiting. A full bar invites completion only after task dependencies are satisfied. Heat shows a labelled outcome bar. Return-heat keeps results inside the task, with no elapsed-time bar.

  4. 04

    Where it leads

    Overview opens the whole task. Inside a unit, the whole card opens that unit’s work; the location footer is omitted.

Resolve the card in this order

  1. Already closed → Completed or Ended early, at the bottom.
  2. Confirmed actionable exception → task-specific work due now.
  3. All required work recorded → check dependencies before offering completion.
  4. Only this unit finished → keep the task open.
  5. No work due → next scheduled event, or a quiet waiting message.

One main number, at most one supporting line. Keep aggregate progress visible while waiting. Counts are recalculated for the selected unit; task completion always checks all units.

Open cards sort: actionable exceptions / active farrowing → overdue → due now → ready to complete → waiting by next time. Equal priorities retain their order.

Not separate progress-tracked tasks

Weight checks, feed adjustments, notes and routine health observations stay in Inspection. Maintenance stays under the unit. Cleaning is removed from this task catalogue.

Further definition needed

Nursery-exit and finishing-exit checks appear in the Today inventory, but lack dedicated task specifications. Their exact completion and timing rules remain open; they are not given invented progress bars here.
