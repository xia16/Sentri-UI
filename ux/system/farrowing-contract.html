C1 · The ledger — events and derivations

One append-only event log per litter session, every event stamped who · when · device. Nothing is ever updated or deleted; corrections append. Two read layers: the event stream (every tap, crash-safe, synced) and the ledger view (History: events netted per type per visit — a same-visit +/− pair renders as nothing).

EventPayloadWritten byEffect
alive_tap{delta:+1|−1} or {set:n} (pad)Alive − / + / type-to-setmoves the open visit's pending sum. The Born floor (audit-corrected): the total clamps at min alive = B* − current dead total, where B* = Born at the last posted count of the living (re-anchored by every alive fold and every Adjust commit) — bodies since the last count unlock the steps down, across visit boundaries. Commits immediately; folds at visit end into one net line. Net 0 → no History line.
dead_tally{type}drawer row +, tail-list pickpending[type] += 1. Types: stillborn · mummified · crushed · scours · starve_out · other (+ farm tail values behind other). Commits immediately.
dead_undo{type}drawer row − (grown-in)pending[type] −= 1. Guards: pending[type] > 0, AND undoing must not strand alive below the Born floor (tally-then-undo was the audit's silent-drop attack) — when it would, this − grays like Alive's. Can never touch posted. Same-visit pair nets to nothing.
visit_fold{alive:{from,to}, dead:{type:+n…}}system, at visit end (C3)pending → posted as ONE stamped History line per figure. Emitted only if net ≠ 0.
correction{deltas:[{figure, from, to}…]}Adjust only (Save)ONE stamped event carrying every changed figure's from→to; each corrected figure wears ✎ forever. A wrong type is two deltas in one Save; a wrong sow is a correction here plus a recording on the right sow (linked mirrored moves retired).

C3 · The visit — the pending window

A visit is one device's open editing window on one session. Every tap commits its event immediately (crash-safe); the visit is only the netting and undo boundary, and its close emits visit_fold.

RuleContract
Opensfirst write-tap on a sheet or drawer (not on open — opening writes nothing).
Ends (folds) ruledon Close · on sheet dismiss (grab swipe / backdrop tap — identical to Close) · on Adjust entry (corrections operate on posted state only; the audit challenges this trigger — C11) · at Lock commit and at session_end · after 90 min idle, measured from the last interaction on the sheet (any tap, not just committed records — a hand standing through a slow farrowing is not idle) draft. Phone sleep / app background alone does NOT end it. A fold under an open sheet repaints receipt and capsules with the standard settle; a control drawn pre-fold acts on post-fold state (a stale − tap is a no-op).
Ends (folds) drafton entering the Finish face — the summary reviews posted truth and the lock must not race a buffer. Mirrors the Adjust rule; awaiting owner confirmation.
Pending, Alivesigned within the floor — negative only against bodies tallied this visit. Receipt reads 5 posted · +4 this visit or 5 posted · −2 this visit (amber −2) undrawn; the receipt never mentions the floor — the grayed − carries it.
Pending, dead typesnon-negative per type (a pile is append-only). − clamps at 0 and withers out.
Undo scopethe pending window is the free-undo window, session-long, never a toast timer. Posted state is only ever moved by Adjust.

The mode flip, stated once: During, Alive is observed and Born derives. After the lock (or end), Born is frozen and alive_now derives. Post-lock dead tallies therefore move alive_now automatically; during, they never touch Alive — the hand converges it separately.

C7 · Sync & offline — the merge contract draft

Rewritten after the attack audit: blanket last-write-wins on absolute asserts let a stale device silently drop Born across phones, fold payloads had no defined merge, and events could arrive after a lock with nowhere to land. The contract below flags what it cannot honestly merge — it never silently drops, never silently applies a breach, and never trusts a device clock to pick a winner.

RuleContract
per-tap commitevery event persists locally at the tap and syncs when online. There is no unsaved state anywhere in the suite; kill the app mid-tap and nothing is lost.
mergeappend-only union; count events merge as signed deltas against their device-visible base — absolute sets decompose to deltas, so two offline observers add information instead of overwriting each other. A merged result that would breach a zero floor or the Born floor is not applied: the figure holds at its floor and a sync review line posts (History + console anomaly tally), resolved through Adjust.
merge × final / session_endevents stamped before a lock/end but arriving after are flagged as sync review and offered through the record's own ceremony (More born › / Count was wrong ›) — never silently dropped (per-tap-commit's promise), never silently applied (the lock's promise). Duplicate finals: earliest stands, later flags.
live synconline, another hand's events land on your open sheet as posted-state changes (your pending rides on top). Room rows, lens counts, census tick in the same frame (motion C).
double-tallytwo hands tallying the same body double-counts the pile — accepted cost, visible via live sync, fixed via Adjust. No locking, no claiming in v1.
stampsevery event carries who · when · device; History and the saved hh:mm · who stamp render them. Entry-time stamping is the compliance grain — no sign-off ceremony exists.

C10 · Invariants and the never-list

#Invariant — violating any of these is a bug
1Born = Alive + Σ Dead + fostered_out − fostered_in at every moment, on every device.
2Born is never directly editable — two observable numbers before the lock, stamped amendment after.
3Every dead piglet is one tally in exactly one type; no − can ever touch posted state — dead rows floor at pending 0 (and at the Born floor), Alive floors at B* − current dead total. Born never sinks below its floor except through Adjust — per device by construction, across devices by the flagging merge. Upward, integrity is provenance-only: every + is stamped; the console owes the per-hand audit views.
4Nothing is deleted; corrections append, stamped, visible as corrections, ✎ forever.
5Corrections to posted state pass through Adjust and nowhere else; why is asked only there.
6Every event commits at the tap; opening/reading/closing writes nothing; registers derive from posted state.
7One resting control per figure; anything not listed as tappable has no affordance.
8Same gesture for every hand: make the screen match the crate.
Never build: Save · submit buttons · success toasts · confirmation dialogs on recording · WHEN/date questions in the drawer · staleness escalations, threshold configs, notifications · dim-as-disabled (absent, never greyed — ONE scoped exception: Alive's − at its floor, owner-ruled) · hard gates anywhere (a blocked path is a falsification incentive) · role gates (v1) · foster during farrowing · scattered correction entrances (floor-doors, tap-the-fact, hidden gestures) · a separate detail screen (the sheet reopened IS the record).
