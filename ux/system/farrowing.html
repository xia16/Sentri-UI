Farrowing

The farrowing room, from a sow going into labor to the batch closing: the standing surface, the session sheet, piglet care by day-age, identity capture, the count, and the close. Requirements and every owner decision live in ux/research/farrowing/SYNTHESIS.md — this document is those decisions drawn.

Nothing here is a new component. The room is the unified list (01), the session is the C6 Session chassis, piglet care is C4 inline tokens, tag/weigh is the C3 Conveyor, the count is the count-subject drawer, and every commit follows the motion contract. Section numbers continue the shared series as 09.

09 · The farrowing room — a standing surface that drains

Not a windowed task: each sow is her own window (expected = service + 114 d, hardcoded). The list is the room view — no dashboard, no grid. And it serves two farrowing models at once: the attended sow whose litter is being tracked birth by birth, and the unattended sow who farrows free and gets her whole litter entered as one batch when the hand comes back. The All lens below shows every register; on the walk the hand lives in Active.

ReceiptsThe first count — or the one-tap started mark — crosses an Awaiting sow to Active: that is "farrowing started". Finish crosses to Done. Sow death or abortion exits every lens; the census ticks (motion C).
Two farrowing models, one row grammar. Attended: the sheet is a live tracker and the row is its running total. Unattended: the room asserts only what a hand asserted — Started · no record yet, or nothing at all until the batch entry. No row ever assumes real-time entry, and the batch path is the same sheet, not a degenerate tracker.
The overdue chip is the free-farrow prompt. Past day 116 the red chip is not only "induce or examine" — on an unattended farm it is the standing reminder that a silent crate may already hold a litter. The prompt costs no notification: it is the row itself, aging in words.

09a · The session drawer — the final model

The sheet is a picture of the crate, drawn light: Alive is one classless append-only count (the framed number types); Dead is five types + Other, tallied as found on borderless rows; Born derives and stands alone. Every downward move lives in Adjust, in the bar. Finish shows the summary, asks any weak or deformed?, and locks Born; after the lock, Adjust survives behind a warning.

Rules — the final model (session-interaction-spec.md)

RuleWhy
A picture of the crateAlive — one classless count, append-only: the + or typing up (tap the framed count). Dead — one side, five types + Other, append-only, tallied as found. Born = alive + dead — derived, shown alone, never entered. A zero row shows a lone quiet +; once tallied, the control becomes the cart capsule − n + — minus exists exactly when there is something to take back, never as a disabled state. Minus is always a took-one-back correction (logged, same-visit pairs net out); a death is never a minus — it is a dead-side tally. Born derives, so no key can touch the fact directly. Born prints at the foot of the sheet, the total under its addends.
Nothing counts down hereBoth sides only grow. Every downward move — mis-taps, miscounts, a counted piglet that died (tally the body, then adjust alive down) — lives in Adjust, in the bar beside Save: a correction surface whose changes log as corrections.
Finish is a summary firstTapping Finish farrowing shows the run — 14 born · 9 alive · 5 dead with the dead breakdown — then asks the one question: Any weak or deformed? Healthy derives as the remainder. Lock born 14 is verb-led, hold-to-commit, separated from Back.
After the lockThe record: read lines, Report death as the one routine action — and Adjust survives the lock, behind a warning that quotes the fact ("Born 14 is locked — adjusting amends the record. Continue?") before opening the same drawer; every change stamps as an amendment.
Every tap landsPress state, count pulse, haptic; a transient inline undo on the tallied row for a few seconds — never a toast. Whole rows are targets; keys are 44 px (48 for the hero); zero rows keep full-ink labels with a grey digit and a quiet key.
No WHENThe stamp is the date; the type carries the timing (stillborn/mummified born dead, crushed/scours died after, Other absorbs the unknowable).

09b · Piglet death after the record closes

Not all piglets make it, and the drawer must cost almost nothing. So the cause cells are the entry: each tap is one death of that cause, the count derives, and Record 3 died writes once. Three crushed overnight = three taps and Record — four touches total. An uncaused death is a tap on Other, a cause like any other. Same drawer everywhere: in the task while it is open, in check-in after.

Rules

RuleWhy
Cause-ledThere is no Died stepper to feed first — tapping Crushed twice and Scours once reads Record 3 died with no other touch. Long-press a cell for − and its stepper. Whole-cell targets, per-visit deltas, same grammar as the session sheet (09a).
Four causes cover the tail that mattersCrushing is typically half or more of pre-weaning deaths; scours and starve-out the next block; Other is honest and legal. Those four are one tap and cover ~95% of records. Other › opens the farm's configured list (savaged, splay-leg …) for the rest — two taps, never a wall of tiles.
No WHENThe stamp is the date (owner ruling). Death dates equal record dates; at daily-walk granularity that is the accepted precision, and mortality-by-day analytics reads stamps. The type itself carries the timing: stillborn / mummified born dead, crushed / scours died after.
One commitRecord 3 died writes once — count, causes, date words — one stamped ledger event. Until then the drawer is scratch; the census ticks on Record (motion C) and the trail reads 3 died · crushed 2 · scours 1 · today · G.H, ✎ forever.
Named pigletsA tagged piglet is never subtracted namelessly — if the count exceeds the untagged remainder, the drawer becomes a roster naming which.

09c · Piglet processing — the day-cohort, not the ladder

The working set is every litter due today, in crate walk order. The per-litter day-age ladder survives only as the litter record's schedule strip — the drill-in, not the workplace. Treatments are tokens on a work line of their own under the litter line — a full day's kit fits without clipping; each token commits for the whole litter, exceptions by count.

Rules

RuleWhy
Token tap = litter markOne tap marks the treatment for the whole litter — advance-in-place: wash + stamp, the token flips to teeth ✓ 08:12, the row never departs. Long-press opens the count stepper: iron · 7 of 11 commits 7, the token reads iron · 4 left, still due. No partial state exists — only a smaller count.
Last token crossesWhen the last due token flips, the row settles (800 ms idle), then group-collapses atop Done — motion B, one reflow. Lens counts and the day gauge tick in the same frame.
Late is not goneAn undone day accumulates oldest-first and prints red: drops · 2d late. Marking late stamps the actual who · when; the on-time KPI absorbs it. No conversion to a health flag in v1 — late work back-fills.
FostersRide the crate's schedule. The reminder is the standing note on the memory line — incl 3 fostered · 2d older — a fact, not a notification; the count grammar above it stays clean.
Catch-upEnd of row, gun down, phone out: checkboxes → bulk mark sheet → one commit, one reflow. Entry-time stamps stand (owner ruling); the day is the compliance grain.
Two handsLive sync — a row the other hand finishes departs your list by the same group collapse. Offline, marks merge append-only; the same mark twice dedupes as same litter · treatment · day.

09d · Tag · notch · weigh — one piglet, one confirm

The heaviest data entry on the farm, so the metric is taps per piglet. The pairing — which piglet got which tag — is captured at the moment the tag goes in the ear, never reconstructed later. tag/weigh › on the litter row opens the C3 Conveyor: fields in capture order, one framed field the pad writes into, the running list as the receipt.

Rules

RuleWhy
Suggested, never managedThe system owns no ranges. The first tag is typed or scanned; after that the field suggests +1 — strips are sequential, so the guess is wrong at most once. Scan (visual code or RFID) always overrides. A duplicate warns amber inline and commits anyway — the tags are the farm's own purchase.
Per-piglet commitThe tag is already in the ear, so the record follows the world at Confirm. Interruption loses nothing; re-entry resumes at 5 of 12 with the suggestion at last +1. Finishing early is legal — keepers-only farms stop at 5 and the rest stay the group count.
The running list001237 · 1.51 kg ♂ ✓ — the receipt, printed at the weight of one. It is also the two-person audit channel: the recorder reads the last line back to the tagger whenever either suspects drift.
Optional everywhereWeight (previous ghosts as placeholder — a hint, never a value), sex, notch. A litter tagged with zero weights is complete. Notch-only farms run the same sheet with the notch as the identity; lookup-by-notch exists wherever lookup-by-tag does.
Fix laterEvery pairing stays viewable and ✎ forever — swapping two piglets' tags is two stamped edits. Nothing seals at submit, because there is no submit.

09e · The count — assertion commits, doors explain

Hands count whenever piglets pass through hands — processing, fostering, weaning. A fresh count that disagrees with the ledger is news that one line is missing, not an anomaly to launder: the count commits at the stepper, and the delta renders as doors.

RuleWhy
The ledger is the truthcount = live at birth − deaths ± fosters — live at birth being the class sum, healthy + weak + deformed. A short count means a missing line, and each door writes it: the death door is 09b's drawer with died 2 pre-filled; the foster door is the pairing with 2 to move. Up-deltas mirror: record births · foster in · just set count. The system never invents or removes a piglet on its own.
Just set countLegal, always — including at weaning. The 18 stands, the trail keeps 2 unexplained, the anomaly tallies on the compliance console, and the transfer never blocks. A gate here manufactures fake deaths; the tally is the honest pressure.
ReceiptThe census ticks on the litter row and the pen header in the same frame (motion C); the trail explains itself: was 20 · 2 died · since jul 12.

09f · Closing the batch — doors, never a wall

Close farrowing = done farrowing this batch. Anyone may close. Sows that died or aborted already left through their own verbs, so the remainder is only sows that have not farrowed — and one of them may never have been in pig at all, which is exactly why closing can never be blocked: a hard gate manufactures fake records; provenance is the audit.

RuleWhy
Anyone closesNo role gate. The close is one more stamped record — who · when. Provenance is the audit.
Remainder = doorsEach unfarrowed sow offers her door (not in pig · abort · next batch) — or close anyway and she escalates: red strip row, watched in check-in, never expiring silently.
No ceremonyNo summary card. The closed run is the task sheet frozen — tallies, who closed, when — reached from Today's done card. The lenses carried the tallies all along; nothing waits for the close.
