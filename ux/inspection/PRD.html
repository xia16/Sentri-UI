巡检 — Inspection

requirements extracted from figma · file 4GZGPBauEOWQQjnRrzoUgF · section 60:408 · ~150 screens across 6 clusters · basis for redesign, not a redesign

This is the second production surface, separate from 生产任务 (production tasks) and in its own Figma file. Where a task is a scheduled sweep with a cohort and a target, an inspection is an unscheduled walk of one unit during which a worker can act on any pig — change her reproductive status, record a measurement, open a health case, move her, report her dead — and inspect the unit's equipment. Nearly every ad-hoc action in the product lives here.

What an inspection is A per-unit walkthrough driven by recency, not scheduling. There is no work order, assignee or due date anywhere in the file. The worker taps 巡检 on home, sees every house stamped 上次巡检：1 分钟前，张强 … 365 天前，张华强, picks one, and lands on a single scrolling worksheet listing every pen with its feed stations and pigs. Ticking rows arms a bottom bar of three sub-menus — 生产 · 健康 · 记录 — which is where all ad-hoc actions live. Closing out is one act, 提交巡检, which asserts 所有栏位与设备已检查完毕 and then optionally collects unit-level environment and biosecurity readings.

3 · Bulk vs single — production's own rule

Two designer annotations state the taxonomy explicitly. Together they cover every sub-function:

853:2119 — 发情、意外妊娠、流产、分娩、断奶：只能作用于 单头猪，选择多头猪时，此按钮禁用 / 标记留种、移除留种标记：可作用于 单头猪或多头猪
Heat, unexpected pregnancy, abortion, farrowing, weaning act on a single pig only; multi-select disables the button. Mark / unmark breeding stock works on one or many.
654:2366 — 体重、背膘、温度：只能作用于 单头猪，选择多头猪时，此按钮禁用 / 转移、寄养、上报失踪：可作用于 单头猪或多头猪
Weight, backfat, temperature act on a single pig only. Transfer, fostering and report-missing work on one or many.
Single-pig onlyBulk-capableWhy (inferred — production never says)
发情 · 意外妊娠 · 流产 · 分娩 · 断奶标记留种 · 移除留种Each reproductive event carries its own timestamp, batch consequence and per-animal payload; the 留种 flag carries none
体重 · 背膘 · 温度转移 · 寄养 · 上报失踪A measurement is a per-animal value; a move shares one destination
—All 5 health actionsConditions, triage and treatment share one payload across a selection; death alone keeps a per-pig payload
Partial eligibility — the rule that matters most When a bulk selection contains pigs that cannot perform the action, production lets it through: 选中的 {N} 头猪只中，仅有 {M} 头猪只可执行此操作，确认…吗？ — confirm is enabled, the operation runs on the M eligible pigs and silently drops N−M. There is no list of which pigs, no reason, no de-select affordance and no partial-result screen. What makes a pig ineligible is never stated anywhere in the file. 654:2397 · 654:2292 · 654:2367

5 · Batch membership gates production

Every production action reads the sow's batch state, and the branches differ per action. An annotation corrects the test: it is not membership but whether the batch still has any of 查情 / 配种 / 分娩 / 断奶 pending 850:2000.

ActionBatch requirementConsequence
分娩MandatoryBlocked outright: 不属于批次不可记录分娩; litter steppers disabled until a batch is chosen
意外妊娠Must NOT be in oneTile disabled if she is already in a batch
发情OptionalCan join a batch, or record only. Confirming 返情 removes her
流产 · 断奶OptionalIf in a batch, removes her — behind a mandatory acknowledgement checkbox

The three near-identical 选择批次 pickers are not duplicates: each applies a different eligibility filter (e.g. 发情 wants 配种任务进行中或尚未开始; 分娩 wants 配种任务已完成并且分娩任务未完成). 选择生产线 exists because batch search is line-scoped.

6 · Requirements risks — contradictions and gaps

Everything here is a genuine ambiguity in the source, not a design opinion. Each needs an answer before the redesign can commit.

1 · Weight has two incompatible models. One set of frames has a required 体重记录方式 switch (总体重 | 个体体重); a later set drops the switch entirely; a third unplaced frame renders it as radio buttons. Nothing arbitrates. Worse, the annotation says weight is single-pig-only while six of seven weight frames show 已选 10 头猪 with a group-weight model.
2 · Ineligibility is undefined. The partial-eligibility dialog exists, but no screen or annotation states what makes a pig ineligible for an operation.
3 · 移除 vs 康复 have no stated difference. They share one screen, one submit and one confirmation; nothing says what distinguishes "remove" from "recovered" as data.
4 · Health triage levels have no semantics. Four levels exist (紧急治疗 · 优先干预 · 常规观察 · 健康) but every description is the placeholder 这里是健康分诊等级的描述.
5 · Fault resolution does not exist. A 解决故障 button is drawn and enabled; no flow, screen or annotation defines it. Both list variants also name their action bar 废弃 (deprecated) while it is the only way to reach fault reporting.
6 · Equipment annotations contradict the current form. An annotation says typed devices need no description and that tapping report-fault is the report; the current sheet requires a description. Node ids suggest the annotation is stale — nothing marks it so.
7 · 提交巡检 asserts what nothing validates. It claims all pens and devices are checked, is offered even from a single-pen drill-down, and gates only optional readings.
8 · The feed-station section is fully designed but marked out of scope (❌ 本期不开发), while 需操作 — a filter chip and the only status line on Grid cards — is defined solely in feed-station terms.
9 · Location hierarchy depth is unsettled — 区/车间/舍/单元/栏位 appears at three different depths inside the transfer flow alone.
10 · Fostering is listed as bulk-capable in the annotation, but the flow picks one counterpart sow. Whether "N" means N piglets or N sows is unstated — and it conflicts with the individual-only treatment fostering gets in the 生产任务 file.

7 · How this relates to 生产任务

生产任务 (tasks)巡检 (inspection)
TriggerScheduled — batch, cohort, target, windowRecency — "last inspected 3 days ago"
ScopeOne task across unitsOne unit, all tasks and none
SubjectThe task's cohortAny pig in the unit, identified or not
Ad-hoc actions更多操作 ⋯ menu — 7 rows, gated by task stageThree sub-menus (生产 · 健康 · 记录) — 17 tiles, gated by selection eligibility
OverlapFostering, death reporting, treatment, transfer and health management appear in both files with different designs. Weaning and farrowing exist as both a scheduled task and an inspection action.

This overlap is the central integration question for the redesign: the same seven actions are specified twice, with different flows, different bulk rules and different gating. The inspection file's rules are more complete (it states its bulk taxonomy in writing); the task file's flows are more detailed.
