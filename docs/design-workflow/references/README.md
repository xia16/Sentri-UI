# House references

The screens the owner points to as the product's taste (2026-10-12). Every builder opens these before the critique and holds each direction beside one of them; every judge looks at them before judging. They are the bar, not the base: a changed screen should look like it belongs next to them. Shot at 390 from `origin/main` with `node scripts/shoot-screens.mjs`; reshoot them when one of these screens is changed and approved.

## What they share

- **Rows, not boxes.** Content lives in white grouped cards split by hairlines; a row is icon tile · title · grey subtitle · chevron.
- **Small headings outside the cards**, regular weight, often with a 16px line icon; the meta pushed right in grey.
- **One hero per card**: a big number (mono for IDs and counts) and everything else small and grey.
- **Colour only for state**: the green progress bar, the amber offline banner, a state word. Everything else is ink and grey.
- **One dark control per screen**: the primary tool (Scan, Finish). Secondary actions are plain rows or outline buttons.
