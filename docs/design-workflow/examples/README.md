# Examples: the owner's eye, shown

The bottom of the taste ladder. [principles.md](../principles.md) and the [rules ledger](../rules.md) carry how we work; this carries what good looks like, as pictures of the owner's rulings. It climbs the same way the rules do:

| Rung | What | Who | Climbs when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ruling** | The owner's pick on a lettered board, or their words on a pair ([rulings.md](rulings.md)) | The owner | It is filed |
| **Example** | That pair cropped on the part that changed, filed under one quality, the owner's words as its caption | An agent files it; the owner can veto the quality | A quality has three new examples, or a judge misread one |
| **Lesson** | `<quality>/lesson.md`: what the examples share, in a few lines (what it looks like, why it fails, what instead) | A distiller agent at consolidation | Lessons from different qualities share a cause |
| **Taste** | A statement in [taste.md](../taste.md) | Consolidation | — |

Only **judgement** rulings climb this ladder: the ones that can't yet be stated exactly (cluttered, bulky, heavy). A **concrete** ruling, one that can be stated or measured (a spacing rule, a component's tap target), goes straight to a design-system law or component spec, with a check where it can be measured; its pair is kept in `ux/design-system/evidence/` as the evidence and the check's test case ([rulings.md](rulings.md)). A lesson that becomes statable leaves the ladder the same way. A check is accepted only when it fails its evidence and passes the [house references](../references/README.md).

## Qualities (fixed)

`cluttered` (too much, everything enlarged) · `flat` (no hierarchy) · `bulky` (boxes, bold and size) · `messy` (spacing, alignment, rhythm) · `disorganised` (facts not grouped by the worker's questions) · `surfaces` (surfaces and colour) · `unclear-state` (what is tappable, selected, unavailable). A new kind of failure fits one of these or replaces one.

## Teaching and held-out

Each quality keeps `teaching/` (builders and the distiller see them) and `held-out/` (never read by anyone; a judge rates them blind to prove the lessons carry the owner's eye). c1–c3 are held-out: taste.md described them, so they can no longer teach. About five teaching pairs per quality; a pair every judge gets right is archived, a pair judges miss stays.

Only the owner's rulings become examples. An agent's opinion never does.
