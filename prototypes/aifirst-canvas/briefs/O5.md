## Build
1. **Equipment** (LocationCard E1): the bar's Equipment opens a white popover of tiles for the current place (3 across): icon in a circle, name, one line of state. On = white tile, dark icon; off = muted tile; out of range = amber; a fault = red state and a red dot. Groups: the room's own devices (Temperature, Ammonia, Climate, Fan, Lights, Drinkers, Camera 1, Camera 2 — cameras are equipment), then "Serving pens" (Feed line "Stopped" fault, Heat lamps "50 of 50 on"). A camera tile opens its feed (reuse what the page has for cameras).
2. **A tile opens its card** (E2) in the same popover: back, name and level, ✕; for the fan: a tall pill to drag (60 %), two choices Auto ("Follows the room climate") and Manual ("Holds the speed you set"), the last change, "Apply 60 %" dark.
3. **A device that serves pens** (E3, heat lamps): its card has "Pick pens"; picking pens switches the farm to Plan (bird's-eye); the pens it serves get a box; clicking a pen ticks it (ink ring + ✓); the bottom bar becomes its control: "Heat lamps · 4 pens", On | Off, − 75 % +, Cancel, "Apply to 4 pens".
4. **The views** (LocationCard "Moving between 3D, Plan and the levels", PLAN.md "Views"): a task that needs pens tilts the camera straight down over the same place, framed to those pens, in one short move (~0.4 s; a quick fade under reduced motion); save the 3D camera on entry and restore it exactly when the task ends (Done, Apply, Cancel, Esc). While Plan is open for a task, a white pill at the top (right of the location card) names it and holds Done (not needed when the bar is the control). Choosing Plan with the 3D | Plan switch stays until switched back. Going to another place ends the task and opens the place in 3D. Esc: closes a popover, then ends the task, then goes up one level. Place and level stay the same when the view changes.
