   - the place's sensors as chips with an icon ("22.4 °C", "61 %", "NH₃ 9.4"); amber when out of range.

3. **A sensor chip clicked:** a small white popover under it: the name, a 24 h | 7 d switch, the value (amber when out of range) with a short reason ("above 8 for 2 h"), a small area chart with the limit dashed.
4. Keep the farm's red ! on a pen with a fault; the card never shows fault text.
