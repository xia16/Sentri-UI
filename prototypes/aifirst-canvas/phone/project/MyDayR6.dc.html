Round 6: My day in Home’s look, work batched with no order, the agent only when there’s something (mock-up)

After your notes on round 5: it was ugly; nothing should show when there’s nothing to know; and the day could be your main tasks with their subtasks batched, not a numbered order. Two ways to batch, in the same look as Home on main (df82ba5d).

Round 6: Home’s look, work batched, no order
  • It looks like Home now: the same header, white cards with an icon tile, a 17 px title over a quiet line. No timeline rail, no numbered circles, no clock chips.
  • No order. Nothing says “first” or “then”. Work is batched: in A under its main task, in B under its place. A fixed time shows only where the work has one (Pen-strep 15:00).
  • The agent speaks only when there’s something the screens and rules can’t show: a violet band at the foot of that card. No band, nothing to know (frame 4). The report opens from the band.
  • Feeding is one quiet line with its times. No card, nothing to open.
A or B?
  • A, by main task (your idea; I’d pick it). The card is the task you open; under it, the batch of things to do inside it. It matches how the app is built (one Farrowing task holds births, fostering and counts) and Home’s groups.
  • B, by place. The card is where you’ll be; the tasks there are rows. It suits the room notes best, but a task that spans rooms shows up twice, and a walk splits.
  • Either way, a subtask line in A is not a door: tapping the card’s head opens the task, where the work is.
The two checks
  • House pattern: Home on main: its header (kicker over a 25 px title), its cards (icon tile, title, quiet line, chevron) and its groups. The report uses Home’s section headings.
  • Attention budget: first, the tasks of the day as cards; second, what to do in each; third, the violet band where there’s something to know. Cut: the order, numbers, rail, clock chips, “first”, the feeding card, any agent line when there’s nothing.
For you to rule
  1. A (by main task) or B (by place)? I’d pick A.
  2. Where do the subtasks come from? I’d take them from the task itself (what’s open in it today: births, fostering, counts, pens with feed to set), never written by the agent, so they can’t disagree with the task page.
  3. Still open: the agent’s name on the phone, and whether the morning brief buzzes. (Auto-tick is moot: My day has no ticks.)
