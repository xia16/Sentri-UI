How farms work it, as far as I know (to confirm with you)
  • Room by room, yes. The animals of one stage share a room, and the work is done there in one visit. Most farms also keep a direction for hygiene: young and healthy before old and sick (farrowing and nursery before finishing, sick pens last), with a boot change between sections. That direction is a farm rule, not the agent’s.
  • Heat check, breeding and inspection go together. The boar is walked along the stalls (usually twice a day); sows standing in heat are bred right there, and each sow is looked over on the same pass. So they’re one room card, three rows.
  • Gestation: the daily look-over is the walk; the pregnancy check is done in the same room visit when sows are due for it.
  • Sourced: the heat check PRD (a twice-a-day sweep, pen by pen). The rest is general farm practice, not yet checked with your farms.
Round 7: My day by room
  • Section, then room. The section (Breeding, Gestation, Farrowing) is a heading; each room is one card, its name as the card’s title; the tasks done in it are rows, each opening its task. No numbers, no “first”.
  • Rooms are listed in the farm’s walking direction, set once for the farm (clean to dirty), not by the agent.
  • Feeding is gone. A time shows only where the work has one (heat check 07:30, Pen-strep 15:00).
  • The violet band at the foot of a room is the agent, only when it has something; it opens that room’s report (frame 3).
