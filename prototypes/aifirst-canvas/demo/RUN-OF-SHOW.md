# Sentri demo · run of show (11 Nov 2026)

**Link:** https://claude.ai/artifact/VbpiYsRUXufeUdccL8TW67. It's private to your Claude account; open it signed in.

**Before customers arrive (2 min):**
- Open the link and wait for the console.
- Type one off-script question, for example "How are the gilts doing?". Your browser asks once to let the page use Claude. Allow it. Live answers run on your account.
- Click **Farm view** once, so the 3D farm is loaded and warm.
- Reload the page to start clean. Every click resets on reload.

The phrases below are rehearsed. They answer instantly and open the right page. Anything else goes to Claude live, which takes a few seconds and can open pages itself.

## 1 · Home (30 s)
- The console beside the chat. Decide rotates every 10 s.
- Click the Decide card to open the item it shows.
- Production / Health / Farm show at a glance; hover any number.

## 2 · Farm setup (2 min, no AI)
- **Farm view** tab: the 3D farm. Click a room to go in; Back comes out. Turn on **Plan**.
- `···` → **Edit layout** shows how the farm is drawn.

## 3 · Production setup (3 to 4 min, the main AI moment)
- Type: **Based on my farm layout, what's the best batch setup?**
- Production setup opens beside the chat. Click **expand** (top right) to read it full width.
- **A batch every 3 weeks**, 50 sows, weaned at 28 days, 35 days in the nursery.
- *"Your two nursery rooms set the pace"*: every room empties, washes and dries between batches.
- **Every room, 12 weeks**: hover a batch.
- **Every rhythm, on your farm**: why weekly, 2-, 4- and 5-week lose (hover each).
- **Your team's three weeks**, then **Set up Line 1**.

## 4 · Tasks in the Farm view (2 min)
- Home → **Task schedule** (or **Day plan**) opens the Schedule.
- Click a task name, for example **Breeding**, **Farrowing** or **Treatment RX-25G009**. Or click a day, then **Open in Farm view**. The Farm view opens on that room with the task's panel open.

## 5 · A batch running late (2 min)
- Type: **Is anything running late?** Or click the Decide card when it shows "Batch 31 is 4 days behind".
- The page shows the weight against plan and the fix (wean Batch 32 four days later), with every move it makes, people included. **Approve**.

## 6 · Treatment from what the team marked (2 min)
- Type: **Any sows need treating?**
- The Vet has drafted treatment for the 4 worst of the 6 sows the team marked lame. Check the Vet's comment on Method, then **Issue treatment**.

## 7 · Whole vaccination programme (3 min)
- Type: **Set up the vaccination programme for the farm**.
- Expand. The Vet drafts six plans one after another:
  - PRRS for the whole herd on fixed dates;
  - the scour vaccine by pregnancy;
  - parvo at weaning;
  - piglets at weaning;
  - gilts on arrival;
  - the antibody check.
- Open each plan, then **Approve this plan**, or **Approve all ready**.

## 8 · At a glance (about 1 min each)
- **Connections:** type *Can I connect my other systems?* → **Accounting** → **Upload an export**. It reads the file, matches the fields, then **Connect**.
- **People:** type *Who are the users and roles?*
- **Decisions:** type *What decisions are waiting on me?*
- **Artifacts** (top right): everything the AI team made today.

## Known rough edges (avoid these)
- **Dates.** Older pages say "Fri 30 Oct"; Home and the new pages say 9 to 10 Nov.
- **Dead buttons.** Home tiles **Finance** and **Initiatives**, composer **+ → Upload a file**, and **New group chat → Create** do nothing.
- **Live answers** vary in wording. If one stalls, ask again. If the browser asked about Claude and you declined, only the rehearsed phrases answer.
