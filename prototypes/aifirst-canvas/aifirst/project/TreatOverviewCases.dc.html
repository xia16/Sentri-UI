Everything above the Pigs list: the header, the Course track, its summary line, the prescription and withdrawal, and whether the page can be trusted. The Pigs list has its own board. Ruled = the owner said so · Proposed = my call, not yet seen · Owner to rule = a conflict with a ruling · Codex = added or changed across three rounds of Codex (GPT) attacks. The last round's three fixes are in but weren't re-checked.

F3StoppingCodexProposed
The confirm names its cutoff and both effects: doses from that moment are cancelled, including today's still open; doses before it keep their outcome, unrecorded ones stay unresolved. Withdrawal still runs from each pig's last dose. A reason is required. A mistaken stop can be reversed by the vet as an audited correction that continues only what was left.
