The day as trips, a rich morning brief, and the heads-up as a notification (mock-up, round 2)

Round 2 after your notes: an interrupted day (a task, a feeding round across five rooms, then back) must read cleanly; the agent writes formatted pages, not chat lines; and its note in a task arrives like a notification you can open or close. Built on the live phone screens from main.

The rule, round 2: a card is a stop, one trip
  • A place: several tasks in one room or block. A round: one task across several rooms (feeding, a walk); its rooms sit on one scrolling line inside, in walking order.
  • Coming back is its own card, named “Back to Farrowing 3”, so an interrupted task reads as leave, round, return; never five room cards.
  • A new card only for a new trip: another place, a round, or a return later. Tasks in the same trip are rows; “as you go” work is a sentence.
  • The console Work schedule follows: a round is one card with its first room and how many more (“Feeding · Farrowing 1 +4”).
The heads-up, round 2: a notification, never forced
  • Opening a task with something to say slides a small card in above the footer: what it’s about and the first line. The page stays usable; nothing is dimmed or blocked.
  • Tap it to read the whole note; ✕ to put it away. Either way the agent’s mark in the header keeps it, with a dot until it’s been read.
  • The agent writes it formatted: a title, one item per pen with the pig named, the one thing to do in bold. Same for the morning brief: a laid-out page, not a chat line.
  • Still short by rule: only what changes what you do today in this task.
