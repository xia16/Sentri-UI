Pages in tabs, the Farm view for where

A task is a page in its tab. Show in Farm view opens the same page in the Farm view pane and lights its pigs where they stand, so you rarely switch back. The chat stays on the left: these frames are the console right pane.
1. Tabs: what and when
Schedule, Batches, a batch, a task, a treatment: pages in their own tabs, with the counts, dates and the full pig list. Each has Show in Farm view; a row's pin shows one pig.
2. Farm view: where
The map, the places and their live state. No Schedule or task panels inside it. Pigs and Devices are layers; Edit layout stays here.
3. The task opens in the pane
Show in Farm view opens the task page in the Farm view pane: icon, title, one line, its progress and its sows. Its pigs light up on their own pens; the rest steps back. ✕ closes it.
4. Pig level lives in the Farm view
Hover a lit pen: her line in the task. Click: the pane goes down to her, her part of the task, her log, her record. Back returns to the task page.
