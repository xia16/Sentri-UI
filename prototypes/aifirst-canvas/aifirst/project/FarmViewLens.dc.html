Pages in tabs, the Farm view for where, joined by a lens

A task stays a page; Show in Farm view carries its progress onto the map, so you rarely switch back. Drawn over a real Farm view screen.
1. Tabs: what and when
Schedule, Batches, a batch, a task, a treatment: pages in their own tabs, with the counts, dates and the full pig list. Each has Show in Farm view; a row's pin shows one pig.
2. Farm view: where
The map, the places and their live state. No Schedule or task panels inside it. Pigs and Devices are layers; Edit layout stays here.
3. A lens joins them
Show in Farm view turns on a lens: a strip under the bar with the task's progress and its legend, the task's pigs coloured on a faded map. Open page goes back; ✕ ends it.
4. Pig level lives in the Farm view
Hover: her line in the task. Click: the pane with her part of the task, her execution log and her record. The pane is for one pig, or one device, nothing else.
