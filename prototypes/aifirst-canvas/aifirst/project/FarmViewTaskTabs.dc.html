Open tasks in the Farm view

How several tasks live on the map at once: tabs on top of the card, one active, collapse to step out, × to close.
1. Tasks add, never replace
Each task opened from a page, the chat or the Schedule becomes a tab on top of the card, left to right. What does not fit goes under +N.
2. One task at a time on the map
The active chip shows its card and colours the map. Clicking another chip switches; the map flies to its rooms.
3. Collapse to leave, × to close
The arrow by ×, a click on empty map or Esc collapses: the map is plain again and the chip stays. Only × on a tab closes a task.
4. Scope follows the bar
Room or pen in the bar: the card counts it. A group pen zooms in and lines its pigs up; a one-sow pen opens her view directly.
