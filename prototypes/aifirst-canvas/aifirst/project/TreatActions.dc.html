Running treatment: the Actions, screen by screen
Proposal. Every change to a running course starts from the "···" menu on its page. Forms (change, add, take off) open in the Treatment tab with Back to the course, in the house form style; short decisions (hold, resume, stop, close) are dialogs over the page. Each ends back on the page, showing what changed. Who may use each action is its own feature (docs, section 11). Mock data: RX-25G009, Gestation 2.
The Actions menu
One "···" beside Show in Farm view. Each item has its icon and a line saying what it does; changes to who and what come first, then the course's state. While held, Hold becomes Resume. People who can't act don't see the menu (Who can do what, in the docs).
