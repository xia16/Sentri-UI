Users: adding people and agents, their access and their seats (product design, before screens)

An admin adds a person with a name, a kind and rooms; the person fills in what they do on their phone. The kind sets the access profile and the seat. Only an admin hires agents and sets their budgets. Prices follow the business model and are placeholders until the April 2027 pilot.

5

Seats and the bill

Small to start, priced by use. The admin sees the cost before adding anyone, and the monthly agent report is the bill.

WhatHow it’s counted
PlatformA small monthly fee; includes the owner’s seat and the General Manager agent
Console seatPer manager or admin, monthly; starts on first sign-in, ends when they leave
Phone worker or tempPer active day: a day with at least one record. A temp stops on their end date
Specialist agentIts own monthly budget: a protected watch part (fixed) and a capped explore part
AI proposalsCharged only when the farm adopts one; anything a fixed rule can do is a free notification
When adding someone: one line under the form, e.g. “Phone, billed by active day: about $X this month at 22 days.” Billing page (admin only): this month so far by line, the agents’ budgets used, adopted proposals. Metering runs in shadow mode before anyone is charged.

Yours to decide

  1. Vets and advisors: a paid console seat, or free while they only read and approve their own items?
  2. Can a manager add workers and temps to their team without an admin? My call: yes; it shows on the bill and on People.
  3. An active day: any record, or just signing in? My call: any record.
  4. Where the agents live in the console: a section on People next to the people, or under Farm settings? My call: People, so the team is one list.
  5. Can a person change their own rooms and jobs after set-up? My call: yes, and a manager sees the change; access stays the admin’s.
