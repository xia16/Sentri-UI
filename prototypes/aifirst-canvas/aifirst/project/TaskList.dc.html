Tasks in Sentri: what is a task, and what is not (reference, 11 Nov)

A task is work the system plans and follows to done, on a group of pigs or a room: it has a list, a progress and an end. Every board names only these. Anything else is an action inside a task, a finding on the walk, or guidance, and is never drawn as a task.
