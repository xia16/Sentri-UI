Group pens: click each pig, each pig its own status (mock-up)

Today a group pen opens a list of IDs while its pigs stay small on the map. Instead, the pen opens on the map and you click the pigs themselves. Nursery 1, pen B7: 9 weaners, two that need a look.

Open: clicking a group pen flies in close; Back, Esc or a click outside the pen flies back to the room.
Status per pig: red pin urgent, amber pin needs a look, no pin fine. From the room, the pen tile stays amber as the summary.
Hover: one line (the ID or “No ID”, and the one thing that matters).
Click: that pig’s panel; ‹ Pen B7 returns to the pen card.
Many pigs: the zoom follows the pen, so 30 pigs zoom closer than 9. Pigs keep their places, so a pile stays a pile; the outline does the picking.
Several at once: Select (top right) ticks pigs one by one; there is still no list.
