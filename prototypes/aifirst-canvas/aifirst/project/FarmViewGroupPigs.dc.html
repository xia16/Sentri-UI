Group pens: the pigs line up, click each one (mock-up)

Today a group pen opens a list of IDs while its pigs stay small on the map. Instead, the pen opens on the map, its pigs line up in rows by status, and you click the pigs themselves. Nursery 1, pen B7: 9 weaners, two that need a look, four without an ID.

Open: clicking a group pen flies in close; Back, Esc or a click outside the pen flies back to the room.
Status per pig: red pin urgent, amber pin needs a look, no pin fine. From the room, the pen tile stays amber as the summary.
Hover: one line (the ID or “No ID”, and the one thing that matters).
Click: that pig’s panel; ‹ Pen B7 returns to the pen card.
Lined up: in rows by status, front to back, heads to the feeder; within a row, by ID. Leaving the pen lets them wander again.
Many pigs: more rows, and the camera comes closer until every pig is at least 40 px long; 30 weaners make five rows of six.
No ID: their own row at the back, a dashed tag, the same click and hover.
Several at once: Select (top right) ticks pigs one by one; there is still no list.
