Pigs list on a treatment: every case
One list for every treatment. A row is a pig with an ID, or a pen's pigs without one; the pen is the farm's label (lane letter + number); the only grouping is the room. Tags and pens are the farm view's own: the sow in B15 is 206950. Ruled = the owner said so · Proposed = my call, not yet seen · Owner to rule = a conflict with a ruling · Codex = added or changed after Codex (GPT) attacked the first board: 27 cards.

Behind the list
ZRules for the developerCodexProposed
Not visible on their own, but every case above depends on them.
  1. A pig has an identity under her tags: tags can change or be added, history stays with her.
  2. Each dose has its own list of pigs it was for. Joining or leaving changes later doses, never earlier ones.
  3. A dose on the track is solid once every pig it was for is settled (given, skipped, taken off), not only when all were given.
  4. A record keeps what was actually given: amount, product, route, time, who, and the prescription version it followed.
  5. Corrections are audited and put the wrong pig back to her true state; the same upload twice counts once; two real doses count twice.
  6. Withdrawal is computed per pig from her actual last exposure, across every course and one-off treatment.
  7. Water and feed courses keep each pen's time on a medicated line, from first access to the flush.
  8. Counts are pigs, not rows. Filters and pages never change the course totals.
  9. The order is fixed (walking order, pen, tag) so paging can't skip or repeat a pig.
  10. A recount of an untagged pen is a stock change; it never edits recorded doses.
