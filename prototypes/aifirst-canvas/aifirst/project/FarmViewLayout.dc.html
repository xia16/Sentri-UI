Farm view, rearranged

A proposal on the farm as it renders today; the 3D is untouched. One rule throughout: nothing on the edges reacts to hover. Hovers stay on the farm (a pen) and on charts where they add detail the screen does not already show. Everything else is a click, and every panel opens in the same place on the right.
1 · At a room: one bar on top, the view tools folded away
The bar moves up beside the location. Select, Batches, Pigs and Equipment each open a main panel on the right; Actions stays the one dark button. 3D, Plan, rotate, zoom, fit, night and Edit layout fold into 3D ▾. The location card has no pigs chip and no breadcrumb, and nothing in it reacts to hover, so it never jumps.
