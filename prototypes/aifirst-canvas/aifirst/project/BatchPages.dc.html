Batches · every page

Grey or white: the convention, then every batch page on it. A tile shows its number and what needs attention; the breakdown is one hover away. The farm view sits behind every panel.
    Grey · anything that holds groups
    The batch panel and every page in it, the pig card, the location card when open, dialogs. Inside, every group is a white card with its heading on the grey above it.
    White · one thing on its own
    A tooltip, a hover card on a pen, a popover or dropdown opened from a control (a filter, Actions from the bar), the bottom bar. No cards inside; rows split by lines. A tooltip is the legend: a header, a keyed row per part, values right, then what a click does.
