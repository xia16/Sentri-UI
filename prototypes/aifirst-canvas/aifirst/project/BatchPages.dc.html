Batches · every page

Desktop panel, 380 wide, as short as it can be. The farm view sits behind it.
  1. Charts and progress come from the atlas: TaskProgress (bar, then counts) and Farrowing Details (against herself, target line).
  2. Desktop adds a few filters (two at most per page); anything deeper goes to the query agent.
  3. Header: back button, the title with its subtitle tight under it, close.
  4. Hover tells, click shows: the counts under a bar are its legend and its filter.
  5. Buttons are soft pills with an icon and one word, equal widths, always at the bottom.
  6. Batch progress is one ring: days done of the stage's planned days.
  7. Running now leads. Upcoming shows at most 3 and is hidden when there are none.
  8. The farm is the pig list; filters live on the location card; equipment faults on the location card.
