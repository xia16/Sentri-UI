Batches · every page

Desktop panel, 380 wide, as short as it can be. The farm view sits behind it.
  1. A batch is its tasks. Clicking a batch opens its page and outlines all its pigs on the farm.
  2. A running task shows progress and its KPI so far. No comparison until it ends.
  3. A done task opens its completion page: the phone's Task outcomes and Performance metrics. Compare lives here.
  4. Outside Compare, pigs live on the farm: hover = her tasks' status, click = her profile.
  5. Inside Compare, the farm compares too: pens tint against the batch, hover = her figure, click = her card beside the batch.
  6. Atlas parts throughout: header, TaskProgress, Performance metrics, the finished sow's Details. At most two filters a page.
