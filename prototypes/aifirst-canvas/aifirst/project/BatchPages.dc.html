Desktop panel, 380 wide, as short as it can be. The farm view sits behind it.
  1. Header: a round back button, the title, close. One muted line under it, then the page.
  2. Hover tells, click shows: no legends, no explaining sentences; small words sit under bars.
  3. Buttons are soft pills with an icon and one word, equal widths, always at the bottom.
  4. Batch progress is one ring: days done of the stage's planned days.
  5. Running now leads. Upcoming shows at most 3 and is hidden when there are none.
  6. The farm is the pig list; filters live on the location card; equipment faults on the location card.
