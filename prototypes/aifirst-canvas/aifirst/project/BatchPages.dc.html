Batches · every page

Desktop panel, 380 wide, as short as it can be. The farm view sits behind it.
  1. A batch is its tasks. Clicking a batch opens its page and outlines all its pigs on the farm.
  2. A running task shows progress and its KPI so far. No comparison until it ends.
  3. A done task opens its completion page: progress, performance, the litter. Compare lives here.
  4. Pigs live on the farm: hovering a pen shows its tasks' status; clicking opens the pig profile. No pig page inside a task.
  5. All dates: done and running tasks open their page. Upcoming on the batch: at most 3, hidden when none.
  6. Atlas parts: header, TaskProgress (counts are the legend and the filter), TaskMetrics. At most two filters a page.
