Treatment on the Farm view

What the hover says and what the task-pig view holds, for a sow and for a group pen. Panes are 320 wide (display). The chat stays on the left.
1. Words from the task
Treatment says Given, Due, Skipped. No generic labels (To do, Needs a look). Colours follow the mobile convention: green done, blue today, amber skipped; tonal fills for shapes, marks for thin parts, ink for words.
2. Hover: one glance
Who, and the one state line in this task (Dose 2 due 08:30). Reasons, counts and history are behind the click.
3. Click: this task, for her
One number, then her doses on a week calendar: Mon to Sun across, a row per week, a small circle per dose. Three doses or three weeks read the same. Hover a circle for that dose. Withdrawal is a chip; Profile pushes her profile in the pane.
4. Group pens
No pen card: the pane stays on the task. Zooming into the pen lines its pigs up in sections by state; tagged pigs click through to their view, untagged ones are counted. Crowded pens shrink the figures; zooming out puts them back
