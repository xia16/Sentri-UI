Treatment on the Farm view

What the hover says and what the task-pig view holds, for a sow and for a group pen. Panes are 320 wide (display). The chat stays on the left.
1. Words from the task
Treatment says Given, Due, Skipped. No generic labels (To do, Needs a look). Colours stay: green given, blue due, amber skipped.
2. Hover: where she is now
Who; her state in this task with when; the recorded reason only if it explains the state. Nothing outside the task.
3. Click: this task, for her
A track of the task's steps, then the log of what was recorded in this task, with who. No moves, no other tasks, no next task: those are on her profile, one button away.
4. Group pens
The same view for the pen: a ring per round (given, skipped), a log that names tagged pigs and counts the rest.
