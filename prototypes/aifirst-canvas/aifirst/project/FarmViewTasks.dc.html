A task on the Farm view

What the map is for when a task is open, the pane it shows, and one quiet rule for pen colours. The chat stays on the left; these frames are the console's right pane.
1. The map helps you find the pens
A task opened on the map answers where the work is and what is left. The full list of pigs stays on the task page.
2. The pane, under a task
Slim: the task, the half circle with its parts as filters, Rooms only when the task spans more than one, Open page.
3. The pen is the unit
One fill per pen; hover tells its part of the task. Click a sow and her profile opens. Click a group pen and the camera flies in: its pigs are arranged by state inside it. There is no pig list on the Farm view; the task page keeps it.
4. Untagged pigs
Counted at the pen, never shown as known individuals: drawn lighter in their state block, with one line under the pen. In big pens only pigs still to do carry tags
