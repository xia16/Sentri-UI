Location card

A supporting card: only where you are, a red dot when something under it is wrong (the farm shows what and where: a broken connected device in 3D, the red ! on the pen), and a few chips: the pigs, the sensors. Hover for the breadcrumb; hover a crumb for its siblings. A sensor chip opens its trend. Equipment and its controls are on the bottom bar: tiles that open a device card; picking pens switches the farm to Plan.
