Work schedule: everyone’s day, suggested by Sentri (mock-up)

Sentri suggests who does which job today and in what order; each person adjusts their own day, in the console and on the phone. The Schedule (Home) stays about when; this is about who and in what order. Hillside Sow Farm, Thu 5 Nov, 09:20.

House pattern
Console: the sibling is the Schedule pop-up (when), so this is the same pop-up shell, grey page and one white card, with people as rows instead of tasks; the job blocks use the Schedule’s colours (blue = now, ✓ = done, red ink = carried over). It opens from Home’s “Your team today”, which already says who is doing what. Phone: its own page beside the to-do list, on the tint with white cards (Surfaces rule), a 56 px Open button, rows with a drag handle.
Attention budget
Manager at 09:20: 1 is anyone overloaded or idle (their day, per row) · 2 what is each person on now (blue) · 3 what is left, in order · 4 what is done (quiet). Worker: 1 what now · 2 then what · 3 can I change it.
Cut: clock times on each job (only the order, morning or afternoon), clock-in times and phone numbers (the People page has them), why each job sits where it does (Sentri speaks only when a move breaks a rule).
How it works (my calls, for you to react to)
Suggested, never approved. Sentri plans tomorrow overnight and again at 05:30 from who is in, the Schedule, each job’s size and the rules. Nobody signs it off.
The rules Sentri plans by: fit each shift; skills (who is trained for each job); whoever gave dose 1 gives dose 2; clean to dirty (piglets, nursery, then sows; the sick room last).
Who changes what: everyone reorders their own day, on the phone or here. A manager can also move jobs between people in their team. The owner can move anything.
When the day goes wrong: someone off sick or a job running long, and Sentri re-plans the rest of the day and says so in the team chat. Nothing lands in the Inbox.
One job, two people: a big job can be split by room (Fostering checks: Farrowing 2 to Li Wei, Farrowing 3 to Liu Yang). Not drawn yet.
The to-do list is untouched: it keeps every task a person can do; My day only puts theirs in order. Ticking a task in either place ticks it in both.
