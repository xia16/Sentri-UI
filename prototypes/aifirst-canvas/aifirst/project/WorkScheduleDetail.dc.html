How farms check rooms, and what the row shows
The law: once a day. UK, EU and Australian rules ask for every pig to be looked over at least once a day by a trained person, and US farm SOPs record that daily check (date, who, where).
In practice: twice. A thorough walk in the morning, a quicker one in the afternoon or evening, usually with feeding. That is the two dots.
While farrowing: every 2–4 h. Around farrowing, rooms are checked every few hours and each birth is noted on the sow card. Only then does the row show more dots.
