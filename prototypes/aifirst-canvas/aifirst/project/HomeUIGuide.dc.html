Home UI guide

How Home is built and why, in one place. 10 Nov. It describes the mock-ups on this page (Home G beside the chat); rulings the owner has made are written as rules, the rest is listed under Still open.

What Home is for

Home shows the farm as it is now, against plan. The daily report explains what changed (board Home and the daily report). Actions are asked for in the chat, never started from Home.

Home sits in the right pane beside the chat. It is read in a few seconds, so every part answers one question and nothing is said twice. When something already shows on another Home card, it is left off this one: the broken fan is in Decide, so it is not on the Farm card.

The dashboard card

Every area’s card is built from the same parts, in this order. A part with nothing to say is left out.

  • Header: the line filter (Production, when the farm has more than one line) or the period (This week, November). › opens the area’s full dashboard.
  • Headline: one number and a short unit (28.6 PSY, 212 sold this week). It turns amber when it is off goal.
  • Trend: 26 weeks as a line with a dashed goal; the goal’s value sits in the right gutter. No axis labels.
  • Figures: up to four. Each is a label, this week’s value and six weeks as bars. A dashed line marks the goal; bars on goal are green, off goal amber, grey when there is no goal.
  • List: one line a row: the batch, a bar for how far along it is, where it goes and a count. Amber when short or over goal.

Never on the card: a title (the tab says it), captions, axis labels, legends (the spend key is the one exception), goal text, dates, a second line on a row, a status chip next to a coloured number.

What hover holds

The card shows the shape; hover gives the words. Everything that was cut from the card lives here, so nothing is lost.

Production lines

  • A farm can run several lines (two sow lines and a finishing line in one farm). Lines are not farms: farms switch at the top left.
  • Together by default: All lines. Lines of the same type add up, weighted by size (PSY 29.2 from 28.6 and 30.4). Mixed lines show what the farm is: sow lines feeding a finishing line make farrow to finish, so the card shows PSY, the sow figures and every batch moving.
  • The filter sits in the header as “All lines” with a small arrow; picking one narrows the card to that line. A farm with one line never sees it.
  • A batch’s line is in its hover, not on the row.
  • Figures follow the stages: sow lines show PSY, farrowing rate, born alive, stillborn and deaths before weaning, then Moving this week. A finishing line shows pigs sold a week, nursery and finishing deaths, days on site and sale weight, then Open batches.

Still open

  • A quiet week: farms that batch every 2–4 weeks will have weeks with nothing moving.
  • The daily report’s format.
  • Health and Farm cards follow the same parts but have not been reviewed on their own.
  • The Work schedule: rooms given to people, read by task (board on the Work schedule page).
