Three directions. One decision.

Three complete brand worlds were built to the same fidelity — same product, same fictional farm, same three screens (Today, Pregnancy check, Farrowing record), same five-status vocabulary. The comparison is therefore about identity, not effort. This sheet scores them against the barn brief — gloves, sunlight, 200-sow lists, workers who navigate by where, not what — and specifies what a final synthesis should absorb and reject from each.

The synthesis

What the final absorbs · what it rejects

From 01 · Ledger — the soul

  • The delta row — struck schedule, colored actual — promoted to system-wide law; slippage is recorded, never scolded
  • The warm-leaning ground: not cream, but neutrals tinted one step toward paper so the app never turns clinical
  • Continuity: Plus Jakarta Sans (text) + IBM Plex Mono (all IDs and numerals) survive from the shipped DS
  • The register voice — calm foreman, numbers attached, no scolding
  • Fraunces — a third family a phone screen can't afford
  • Full warm-cream ground — costs amber its headroom

From 02 · Panel — the machine

  • The barn schematic as the signature home gesture — location-first drawn literally, tap a room to enter it
  • Exception-first layout law: red may only occupy the first slot; a quiet farm shows an almost colorless screen
  • All numerals mono & tabular — counts, tags, times — cheap to build, hard to copy
  • Gauge-segmented progress in the task's status hue
  • Full condensed-caps voice — warning-poster risk in a husbandry app
  • Cool clinical housing as the default ground

From 03 · Board — the spine

  • The status rail: rightmost column is sacred — status word + timestamp, nothing else may cross it, aligned on every screen
  • The color law, stated as a rule of the brand: color may mean status; color may not mean anything else
  • The package-tracking spine for lifecycle timelines (farrowing intervals, sow history): past filled, present colored, next hollow
  • Row grammar: one row = tag · state · rail, 50px, repeated without variation
  • Words-only status (fails low-literacy — rail words get a fixed status glyph + color, so shape carries meaning too)
  • Total mono — data is mono, sentences are not
Recommendation

Panel wins the brief; Ledger wins the heart; Board wins the spine. Scored against the barn — gloves, glare, 200 sows, workers who read position and color before words — Panel is the strongest single direction and the only one whose signature (the schematic) is also the product's core argument: location first. The recommended final is Panel's structure with Ledger's temperature and Board's discipline: schematic home + exception-first law + mono numerals, on a one-step-warm neutral ground with the shipped Jakarta/Plex-Mono pair, governed by Board's color law and status rail, with Ledger's delta row as the system-wide record gesture. Gauge teal vs. shipped olive as the single brand hue is the one open identity question this sheet does not decide — it deserves a two-swatch test on the synthesized screens.
